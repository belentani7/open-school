# Module 4: Content Generation — Level 01
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Seed
## Estimated time: 2 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Know what a chatbot or "writing assistant" is and what it is for.
- Open a text AI tool (for example, ChatGPT or Gemini) without fear.
- Write a simple request and receive a text written by the machine.
- Check that AI does not always get it right and that you must review what it writes.
- Ask for a second version when you do not like the text.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Chatbot | A program you talk to by typing or by voice. It is like a conversation partner. |
| Text AI | An artificial intelligence that creates written words: sentences, letters, lists or poems. |
| Prompt | The request or question you write to the AI. It is like ordering a dish in a restaurant. |
| Response | The text the machine gives back to you. |
| Chat | The window where you write and read the conversation. |
| Conversation window | The place where the AI "remembers" what you have been telling it. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Imagine you walk into a bakery and ask for a loaf of bread. The baker listens and hands it to you. Something similar happens with text AI: you write a request and the machine gives you back a text. The request is called a "prompt", and the text it returns is called a "response".

There are several free text AI programs. The best known are ChatGPT and Gemini. They work on the phone and on the computer, through a website or an app. They are like a friend who never gets tired of writing and answers right away.

To begin, you only need three things: an account (or to go in without an account, depending on the program), a conversation window, and a clear request. The request can be as simple as: "Write me a birthday greeting for my sister".

AI does not write perfectly the first time. Sometimes the text comes out too long, other times too serious or too strange. It is important to understand that the machine is a helper: the person is in charge. If you are not convinced by the text, ask for another version.

How do you ask for another version? Very easy: in the same conversation, write "make it shorter", "make it warmer" or "change the ending". The conversation window keeps what you said, so the AI remembers the previous text and modifies it.

This is like ordering a suit from a tailor. First you are measured (the first request), then the tailor suggests a design (the response), and if you do not like it, it is adjusted. Each "make it shorter" is one more adjustment.

It is a good idea to talk to the AI as you would talk to a person: with complete sentences and good manners. You do not need to write perfectly: the machine understands mistakes. But the clearer the request, the better the result. "Write me a note to my doctor asking for an appointment" works better than "note doctor".

A very common mistake is to believe that everything the AI writes is true. It is not. The AI puts together words that sound good, but it can invent data, names or dates. That is why you should always review the text with a person's eyes, not a machine's.

Another important thing: do not write sensitive personal data (ID number, bank accounts, passwords) in the conversation. The AI is not a private notebook. What you write is kept by the company that runs the program.

The first contact can be a bit intimidating. That is normal. We recommend starting with something unimportant: "Give me three gift ideas for my husband", "Write me a joke", "Help me write the shopping list". Try it and you will see it is simple and even fun.

When the AI responds, you can copy the text and paste it into a message, a letter or a document. To copy, touch the text and look for the "copy" option; then paste it where you want by holding your finger over the spot.

Text AI can also help you improve what you have already written. You can paste a draft of a letter and say "correct it" or "make it more formal". It is like having a personal proofreader available at any hour.

The key to this level is experimenting. The more conversations you have with the AI, the more natural it will feel. There is no exam, there are no serious mistakes: it is all practice. And remember: the machine suggests, you decide.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The note for the doctor.** Rosa asks the AI: "Write me a message to book an appointment at the health centre". She receives a clear text, copies it and sends it through the health website.
2. **The family group message.** Pedro writes: "Make me a Christmas greeting for the family group". The AI suggests a warm text and Pedro sends it just as it is.
3. **The shopping list.** Carmen asks "make me a shopping list for a week of stews". The AI suggests ingredients and Carmen ticks the ones she is missing.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Open the browser on your phone or computer (Chrome or Firefox).
Step 2: Type "chat.openai.com" or "gemini.google.com" in the address bar and press enter.
Step 3: If it asks for an account, create one with your email and a password you will remember. Write the password on a piece of paper you keep safe.
Step 4: Find the box where you type, usually at the bottom of the screen, with the text "Type a message".
Step 5: Write: "Write me a thank-you note to my neighbour who gave me tomatoes from her garden".
Step 6: Press the arrow or the "Enter" key and wait a few seconds. The machine will write a text.
Step 7: Read it calmly. If you like it, copy it. If not, write below: "Make it shorter" and press enter again.
Step 8: When you have the text you want, copy it and paste it into a WhatsApp message to save it.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is a prompt? a) A program. b) The request you write to the AI. c) A photo app.
2. Where do you write the request to the AI? a) In the chat message box. b) In the gallery. c) Nowhere.
3. Is everything the AI writes true? a) Yes, always. b) No, you have to review it. c) Only if it is in English.
4. If you do not like the text, what do you do? a) Close the program. b) Ask for another version in the same conversation. c) Print it anyway.
5. Can you write your password in the AI chat? a) Yes, it is safe. b) No, better not share sensitive data. c) Only if it is short.

Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- Text AI can make mistakes and invent data. Always review what it writes before sending it to someone.
- Do not share sensitive personal data (ID, banks, passwords) in conversations with AI.
- If you are going to use the text for work or an official procedure, pass it through your own judgement and correct it.
- AI has no emotions or opinions of its own: it reflects what has been written on the internet. Do not treat it as an authority.
- Be transparent: if an important text was written by AI, you can say you used it as help. Honesty costs nothing.

## 🧠 Summary and bridge
Text AI is a helper that writes when you ask it clearly. You write a prompt, receive a response, review it and, if it does not convince you, ask for another version. The machine suggests, the person decides. In the next level we will practise with useful everyday texts: letters, messages and lists.
