# Module 4: Content Generation — Level 03
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Seed
## Estimated time: 2 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand that AI can create brand new images from a description.
- Get to know a free image generator (for example, Bing Image Creator) and use it.
- Write a simple description in words and get an image.
- Compare the generated image with what you had imagined.
- Decide whether the image is suitable to use, and where.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Generated image | An image created by a machine from a written description. |
| Image generator | The program that turns words into drawings or photographs. |
| Description | The sentence in which you explain what you want to see. It is like ordering a dish from the cook. |
| Style | The look of the image: realistic photo, drawing, painting, cartoon. |
| Result | The image the program gives you. |
| Save image | Download the image to your phone or computer to keep it. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Have you ever wanted an image that does not exist? For example, a drawing of your house with flowers on the balcony, or a portrait of your pet wearing a pirate hat. Before, to get an image like that you had to pay a designer. Today, artificial intelligence can create it in seconds: you describe what you want and it draws.

The program that does this is called an "image generator". The easiest one to use is Bing Image Creator, which is free and works in the browser. Others include the ones from Canva and Google. They all work the same way: you write a description and the machine hands you an image.

The description is the most important part. Just like with text, the clearer it is, the better the result. If you only write "a dog", the AI will give you any dog. If you write "a brown Labrador dog sitting in a garden with sunflowers", the result will look much more like what you imagined.

It is a good idea to describe three things: the subject (what appears), the setting (where it is) and the style (how it is drawn). For example: "a smiling grandmother" (subject) "in her kitchen wearing an apron" (setting) "oil painting style" (style). It is like giving the painter all the instructions before he starts.

The first image is almost never perfect. The AI may give a person three arms or make the writing look odd. That is normal: the machine still makes mistakes. The good news is that you can try again: change one word of the description and generate again.

You can also ask for variations. Some generators have a button to create different versions of the same image. It is like taking several photos in the same place and keeping the best one.

AI can imitate many styles: realistic photography, watercolour, children's drawing, comic, classical painting. You can say "photography style" or "pencil drawing style". Experimenting with styles is great fun and costs nothing.

Careful: AI can draw things that are not real. An impossible landscape, a house that does not exist, an invented person. This is an advantage for imagination, but also a warning: not everything the AI generates is a real photo. We will look at this in more detail in advanced levels.

What can we use generated images for? To decorate a document, illustrate a recipe, make a birthday card, bring a family story to life, create a club logo, or simply for fun. We use them as we would use any image.

When you save an image, it becomes just another file on your phone or computer. It is usually saved in the downloads folder or the gallery. Then you can send it by WhatsApp or paste it into a document, just like with camera photos.

It is important to read the generator's terms before using images for commercial purposes. Many free tools allow personal use without a problem, but to sell or publish in a business, it is wise to read the small print. We will look at the ethics of use in the next level.

The key to this level is playing. Create images for yourself, without pressure. Try changing the words of the description and watch how the drawing changes. It is like doing magic with words.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The birthday card.** Marta writes: "a three-tier cake with candles, in a garden, cheerful drawing style". She gets an image and uses it to wish her granddaughter a happy birthday.
2. **The recipe illustration.** Jorge asks for "a plate of lentil stew with bread, watercolour style". He saves the image and adds it to his favourite recipe.
3. **The pet portrait.** Ana describes "my black and white cat wearing sunglasses, comic style". She saves the result and sends it to the family group, which has a good laugh.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Open the browser and type "bing.com/images/create" in the address bar.
Step 2: If it asks you to sign in, log in with your Microsoft account (the email one).
Step 3: Find the box that says "Describe the image you'd like to see" or similar.
Step 4: Write this description: "an orange cat sleeping on a sofa next to a window, watercolour style".
Step 5: Press the create button and wait a few seconds.
Step 6: Look at the result. Does it look like what you imagined?
Step 7: Change one word (for example, "watercolour" to "realistic photography") and create again. Compare.
Step 8: Choose the image you like best, press the download or save button and share it on WhatsApp with a relative.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is an image generator? a) A printer. b) A program that creates images from a description. c) A photo camera.
2. What three things should you describe? a) Subject, setting and style. b) Brand, price and size. c) None.
3. Does the first image always come out perfect? a) Yes, always. b) No, many times you have to repeat. c) Only in colour.
4. Can the AI imitate styles like watercolour or comic? a) Yes. b) No. c) Only black and white.
5. Is every AI-generated image a real photo? a) Yes. b) No, it can draw invented things. c) Only the ones of animals.

Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-b, 4-a, 5-b.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- An AI-generated image is not a real photograph. Do not present it as if it were a real photo.
- Do not generate images of real people in false or embarrassing situations: it can cause real harm.
- Check the program's terms before using images for a business or to sell.
- If you share a generated image, you can say the AI made it. Transparency avoids misunderstandings.
- Do not use images to deceive, scam or defame anyone.
