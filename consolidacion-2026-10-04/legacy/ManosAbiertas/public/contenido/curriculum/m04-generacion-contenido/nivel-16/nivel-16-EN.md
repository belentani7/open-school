# Module 4: Content Generation — Level 16
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Branch
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what visual identity is: the "face" of our content.
- Choose colours, fonts and a style that represent us.
- Create a small brand image for the club, the family or your own project.
- Use AI to propose colour palettes and styles.
- Apply the visual identity to photos, videos and documents.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Visual identity | The set of colours, fonts and styles that make content recognisable. |
| Colour palette | The group of colours we always use, like the clothes of a house. |
| Typography | The style of the letters (for example, round or square). |
| Logo | A symbol or drawn name that identifies a group. |
| Consistency | That all content has the same appearance. |
| Brand | The image that others associate with our work. |

## 📚 Main lesson
When we see many videos or photos from one place and they "look like they are from the same place", that is visual identity: the recognisable face of content. Just as we recognise a person by the way they dress, we recognise a project by its colours, its fonts and its style. Visual identity is not a luxury: it is the way for us to be recognised.

Visual identity is made of simple pieces. The first is the colour palette: choosing two or three colours and always using them. For example, the gardening club can use green and earth. The second is the typography: the letters. Round letters convey warmth; straight ones, seriousness. The third is the image style: bright, warm, with afternoon light.

You do not need to be an artist to create a visual identity. AI can help us: we ask it "propose a 3-colour palette for a gardening club for older people" and it suggests combinations. Then we choose the one we like most. AI also proposes font styles and templates. It is like having a designer at hand.

Consistency is the golden rule. It does not mean everything is the same, but that everything matches. If we always use the same colour in titles and the same font, in the end people identify our videos without seeing the name. Consistency builds trust: we know what to expect.

The logo can be very simple: the club's name with a nice font, a drawn leaf, a photo. You do not need to pay a designer. Free design tools (like Canva) have ready-made templates. Remember: a simple, clear logo is worth more than a complicated one that cannot be understood.

Applying visual identity is easy if we define it first. Before making a video or a document, we ask: what colours do I use? what font? what photo style? With the answers written on a single sheet, all content comes out similar. That sheet is called a "style guide" and it is our visual recipe book.

Templates help a lot. In Canva or design programs we can save a poster, presentation or video template with our colours. Every time we use it, the result already has our face. It is like having the biscuit mould: every biscuit comes out the same.

Visual identity also applies to videos: the cover, titles and subtitles with our colours. A video with a recognisable cover looks more professional and makes people want to click. The detail of the colour in the title is already identity.

Over time, visual identity grows. We can change colours if the project changes, but always slowly and carefully, so as not to confuse people. Changing your face suddenly is like moving house without telling anyone: nobody finds us.

Respect and consistency are also ethics. Let us not imitate the identity of another group or association to look like them. Our brand must be ours, with our name and our values. Visual identity is honesty: it shows who we truly are.

By the end of this level, we will know how to create a simple visual identity and apply it to our content. We will have taken a huge step: from making scattered content to building a project with its own face.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Choose a project: the club, the family or your hobby (garden, sewing, photography).
Step 2: Open the AI chat and write: "Propose a 3-colour palette for [your project] and a font style that is [warm/serious/cheerful]".
Step 3: Choose the palette you like most and write down the 3 colours on paper or in a document.
Step 4: Open Canva (or the design tool from class) and look for a poster or presentation template.
Step 5: Change the template colours to yours.
Step 6: Write your project's name in large and choose the font that represents you.
Step 7: Save the template with a clear name ("Club template") to reuse it.
Step 8: Create a poster or a video cover with that template and show it to a classmate.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- Do not copy the visual identity of another association or company: the brand must be yours.
- If you use other people's templates, respect their terms of use.
- Be honest: visual identity must show who you truly are.
- Do not use symbols of institutions or brands without permission in your content.
- Take care of accessibility: the colour contrast must allow everyone to read.
