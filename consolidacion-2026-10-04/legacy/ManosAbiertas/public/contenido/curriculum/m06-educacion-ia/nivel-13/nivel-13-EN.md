# Module 6: Education with AI — Level 13
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Stem
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand why languages are learned best by speaking and listening.
- Learn to practise conversation with AI as if it were a person.
- Use AI to correct our pronunciation and our vocabulary.
- Ask for useful phrases for daily life: travel, family, shopping.
- Practise a complete dialogue from start to finish.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Explanation in simple words |
|---|---|
| Language | A tongue: the way people speak in a country or region. |
| Conversation | Talking with another person (or with AI) exchanging sentences. |
| Vocabulary | The set of words we know in a language. |
| Pronunciation | How words are said out loud. |
| Dialogue | A written or spoken conversation with turns: one says, the other answers. |
| Translation | Moving a sentence from one language to another, like changing currency. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Learning a language is like learning to swim: knowing the rules on paper is not enough, you have to get into the water. With AI we can get into the water of the language every day, without fear and without shame. It is like having a very patient foreign friend who never laughs at our mistakes.

The best gift AI gives us for languages is conversation practice. We say to it: "Let's practise English. Be a friend who speaks English. Greet me". And AI answers in English. We answer as best we can, and it understands us and encourages us. It is like having a language exchange at home, at any hour.

The key is telling AI our level. If we lie and say we know more than we do, the conversation escapes us. Better to be honest: "I am a beginner, speak slowly and with short sentences". AI adapts, like the swimming instructor who starts in the small pool.

We can ask it to correct us kindly: "Correct me when I make mistakes, but without interrupting too much". AI will note our errors and tell us at the end. It is like someone recording your way of walking so you can see what to correct: first you let go, then you look, then you improve.

Pronunciation is also practised. AI can write how a word sounds, or use voices (like the ones we will see in the video levels) to say it. We repeat out loud. It is like singing a song following the singer: the voice sets the rhythm and we follow it.

A very useful trick is to ask for useful sentences. We say: "Give me 10 sentences for ordering food in a restaurant". AI gives them with their translation. It is like carrying the country's recipe book: ready-to-use sentences. We copy them into the notebook and review them with cards (level 12).

To avoid being overwhelmed, we practise with small dialogues. AI proposes a situation: "You are at the train station". It plays the ticket seller and we play the traveller. A ten-line dialogue is a victory. It is like putting on a play at home: each dialogue is a show.

Vocabulary is learned by fields. Let us not learn a hundred loose words: better to learn the kitchen ones, or the family ones, or the shopping ones. AI organises vocabulary for us by topics, as the pantry is organised by shelves. Each shelf is a group of words that go together.

The memory, which we already trained, also comes into play. After each conversation, we ask: "Make me 5 cards with the new words from this conversation". Thus, what we talk about becomes spaced review. It is like keeping the leftovers: they are not thrown away, they are kept for another day.

A common mistake is studying grammar and not speaking. Grammar is the scaffolding, but the house is built by speaking. With AI, let us speak from day one, even if it is with two words. Every spoken sentence is a laid brick. Perfection will come on its own, with time and practice.

We can also use AI to translate what we need in the moment. A sign, a note, a message: "What does this say?". Translation gives us a hand on the street, like the pocket dictionary of old. But let us remember: for important things, a translation can have nuances; let us check when the occasion deserves it.

Language is learned with the repetition of daily life. That is why AI is perfect: it is available whenever we want. A five-minute conversation with the coffee, another while we wait for an appointment. Little by little, the language becomes natural, like the mother tongue we learned without noticing.

Let us not forget the pleasure of learning languages: it opens doors, brings us closer to other people and other countries. With AI, that door opens from home, without paying for expensive classes. It is an opportunity worth taking.

In this level we will live a complete conversation in another language with AI: greeting, introduction, a few sentences, correction and cards. If you do not know any language, you can start with English sentences, which is the most widespread. Each small dialogue will be a step towards fluency.

Let us remember that making mistakes while speaking is the sign that we are learning. Shame is the fear of failing, and AI does not judge: it corrects us and we continue. With such a patient companion, nobody can say that a language is not for them.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Open AI and choose a language (for example, English) and your level (beginner).
Step 2: Write to it: "Let's practise English. I am a beginner. Speak with short sentences and slowly".
Step 3: Ask it to start: "Greet me and ask me how I am".
Step 4: Answer with what you know, even if it is one word: "Fine, thank you".
Step 5: If you do not understand, write: "What does that mean? Tell me in English".
Step 6: Ask for the correction at the end: "Correct the mistakes I have made and tell them to me kindly".
Step 7: Ask for 3 useful sentences from the dialogue and turn them into review cards.
Step 8: Close with a goodbye in the language: "Goodbye, see you tomorrow".

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is best for learning a language? a) Speaking and listening, even if you fail. b) Only reading grammar. c) Not practising.
2. What do we tell AI so the conversation goes well for us? a) Our real level. b) That we know a lot. c) Nothing.
3. If we do not understand a sentence, what do we do? a) We ask for the translation. b) We stay silent. c) We leave.
4. What do we do with the new words from the conversation? a) We turn them into review cards. b) We forget them. c) We ignore them.
5. Is it okay to make mistakes when speaking? a) Yes, it is a sign of learning. b) No, better to be quiet. c) Only if nobody is looking.

Answers: 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- Do not use AI to do other people's language homework or to cheat in exams.
- Automatic translations are not perfect: for important texts, confirm them.
- Respect languages and cultures: learning a language is opening up to others, not mocking them.
- If you talk to AI in another language, do not share personal data: it is training, not friendship.
- Language is shared: when you learn, help others practise, as AI has helped you.
