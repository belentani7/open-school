# Module 6: Education with AI — Level 20
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Canopy
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what the augmented classroom is: the real classroom expanded with AI tools.
- Learn to use AI as an assistant for the teacher or for whoever teaches.
- Prepare activities, materials and questions for a class with AI.
- Learn how AI helps answer doubts and personalise attention.
- Practice the first teaching session with the support of AI.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Explanation in simple words |
|---|---|
| Augmented classroom | The usual class, enriched with AI help, like a kitchen with new utensils. |
| Teacher's assistant | The AI that helps prepare and give the class, without replacing the person. |
| Class plan | The sheet with what will be done in the session: what, how and how much. |
| To personalise | Adapting the teaching to each person, like a made-to-measure suit. |
| Feedback | Telling the student what they did well and what to improve, like a taste test. |
| Facilitator | Someone who helps others learn, more than dictating: someone who brings knowledge closer. |

## 📚 Main lesson
We have learned a great deal: to study, to create materials, to make videos and to play while learning. Now comes the time to turn the tables: use all of that to teach others. We begin with the augmented classroom, which is the usual class, but with AI as an invisible helper. It is like grandmother's kitchen, but with a fridge full of ready-prepared ingredients.

What is the augmented classroom? It is the real classroom, with real people, where AI enters to help. It does not replace the teacher or the relationship between people: it empowers it. AI prepares the materials, suggests activities, solves doubts and frees the teacher's time to attend to those who need it most. It is like having a helper in the kitchen: the cook's hands are still the ones making the stew.

The first job of the augmented classroom is to prepare the class plan. We ask AI: "I have a [topic] class for [audience] of [duration]. Make me a class plan". AI returns the sheet to us: what to say at the beginning, what activities to do, how long each part will last and how to close. It is like the day's task list: we know what comes at each hour.

The class plan is the backbone. Without a plan, the class is a conversation that disperses; with a plan, it is a path that reaches its goal. AI helps us make sure the path has a beginning, a development and an end, and that each part has its fair time, like the dishes of a full menu.

AI also makes the class materials: the worksheets, the questions, the games. We already know how to ask for them from previous levels. For the augmented classroom, we ask for material "for class": "Make me a worksheet to hand out in class" or "a game to play in teams". Everything ready to print or project.

During the class, AI can be the teacher's second voice. If a person asks something we do not know, we can say: "Good question, let us ask our helper". And AI answers. This way, the teacher never runs out of answers and the classroom becomes a place where every question has a home. It is like a dictionary that is always on the table.

The augmented classroom personalises: AI lets us give each person what they need. A student who moves fast receives harder challenges; one who moves slowly receives more simple exercises. We ask: "Prepare 3 difficulty levels for this activity". This way, each person works on their own step, as we already saw in the personalised exercises.

The evaluation also changes. Instead of one exam that is the same for everyone, AI proposes varied tests and corrects them with explanations. But careful about something important: the evaluation must be fair. AI helps to correct, but the decision about the person is always human. The machine proposes, the teacher disposes.

The teacher's role changes with the augmented classroom: from dictator to facilitator. The facilitator brings knowledge closer, encourages, accompanies and decides. They do not compete with AI: they use it. It is like someone who teaches swimming: they do not force the student into the water, but give them a hand and teach them to float.

The augmented classroom is not only for schools. A club meeting, a talk in the neighbourhood, an afternoon of stories with the grandchildren: everything can be an augmented classroom. With AI we prepare the session and answer the questions; people bring the attention and the joy. Teaching becomes possible in any corner.

We must take care of privacy in the classroom. We do not share with AI the names or personal data of the students. We ask about the topic, not about their lives. AI is a classroom tool, not a people archive. That boundary protects us all.

We must also take care of the human relationship. AI answers, but the affection is put there by people. A class is not only information: it is company, encouragement, trust. AI cannot give a pat on the back or see a look of doubt. That is why the augmented classroom needs the human teacher more than ever.

In this level we will prepare our first augmented class: a topic, a plan, some materials and a game, all with AI's help. Then we will put it into practice with someone, even if it is one person, and we will see how technology makes us better teachers. It is the beginning of the Canopy band: teaching others.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The club's class.** Pedro asked for a 40-minute class plan on "boots and mountain safety". AI gave him: a 10-minute talk, a team game and questions. The class was a success.
2. **The live help.** Ana did not know how to answer whether "wholemeal flour is healthier". She asked AI in the class and it answered with the truth, without inventing. The classroom learned to trust the help.
3. **The three levels.** Marta prepared the same activity with 3 difficulty levels. Each person in the workshop worked on their own step and nobody got bored or frustrated.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Choose a topic you want to teach another person.
Step 2: Ask AI: "Make me a [X]-minute class plan about [topic] for [audience]".
Step 3: Review the plan and adjust what does not convince you.
Step 4: Ask for the material: "Make me a worksheet and a game for this class".
Step 5: Prepare 3 difficulty levels: "Give me the activity in 3 levels: easy, medium, difficult".
Step 6: Give the class to one person or a small group.
Step 7: During the class, if there is a difficult question, say: "Let us ask our helper" and use AI.
Step 8: At the end, ask the learner what they liked and what they would change. That answer improves your next class.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is the augmented classroom? a) The usual class, enriched with AI. b) A class without people. c) A new building.
2. What does AI do in the augmented classroom? a) Helps the teacher, without replacing them. b) Replaces the teacher. c) Does nothing.
3. What is the class plan? a) The sheet with what will be done in the session. b) An exam. c) A price list.
4. Who decides about people in the evaluation? a) Always a person. b) Only the machine. c) No one.
5. What can AI do in the classroom? a) Answer doubts and prepare materials. b) Give affection. c) Give final grades on its own.

Answers: 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- AI is an assistant, not an owner: decisions about people are human.
- Protect privacy: never share students' personal data with AI.
- Review what AI proposes before taking it to the classroom: you are responsible for what you teach.
- Do not take an AI advice on health or money as valid without checking it.
- Affection, encouragement and trust are human: do not delegate them to the machine.

## 🧠 Summary and bridge
The augmented classroom is the usual class empowered by AI: class plan, materials, games and answers to doubts, all with the machine's help. AI does not replace the teacher: it frees them to attend to people. The evaluation remains human and privacy is protected. In the next level we will expand the classroom: we will use AI so that education reaches all people, with accessibility and inclusion.
