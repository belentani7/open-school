# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 03
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Seed
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what a chatbot is and how it differs from a search engine.
- Recognise ChatGPT and Gemini as conversation assistants that answer your questions.
- Learn to start a conversation and ask your first questions.
- Understand that the chatbot does not "know" but "calculates" the most probable answer.
- Lose the fear of the white box: nothing you write can break anything.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Chatbot | A computer programme that talks to you in writing, like a person who responds at the other end of the counter. |
| Conversation | The written exchange between you and the chatbot, like a WhatsApp chat but with a machine. |
| Answer | The text the chatbot writes in response to your question. |
| Artificial intelligence (AI) | A machine that has been trained with many examples to do things that require intelligence, such as understanding and writing. |
| Model | The "brain" of the chatbot: a huge mathematical calculation that has learned from millions of texts. |
| Prompt | The question or instruction you write to the chatbot. |

## 📚 Main lesson
A chatbot is a programme with which you talk in writing. You write a question, and it answers you. It is like WhatsApp, but instead of your cousin, on the other side there is a machine trained to help you. The two most famous are ChatGPT, made by the company OpenAI, and Gemini, made by Google. Both are free in their basic version and both are now part of our daily lives.

First of all, let us clear up a very common confusion. The search engine, such as Google, looks for pages that exist and shows them to you. The chatbot, on the other hand, writes a new answer with its own words, made for you, at that moment. It is the difference between showing you the cookbook and cooking a dish for you. The search engine finds the recipe; the chatbot prepares it and explains it to you.

How does the chatbot work? Imagine a very patient assistant who has read millions of books, newspapers and conversations, and has learned how people write. When you ask something, he "calculates" the words that most probably go together to answer you well. That is why the answers are so natural: he has read so many texts that he imitates human language perfectly. But do not be fooled: he does not "know" in the human sense, he calculates probabilities. Sometimes he is wrong, and that is very important to know.

Let's see how to open them. ChatGPT lives on the page chatgpt.com and Gemini on gemini.google.com. If you have created your account as we learned in the previous level, you can enter. There you will find a white box with the phrase "Write a message". That box is your invitation to talk. Write anything, press the enter key or the arrow, and wait a few seconds.

What can you ask? Everything that is done with words. "Explain to me what a mortgage is in simple words." "Suggest a menu for four people with little money." "Write a message to my daughter telling her I love her." "How do I cook lentils so they don't stick?" The chatbot is like an assistant that never tires: it answers you always, however many times you ask, and it never gets angry.

The most important thing at this level: write your question as if you were talking to a person. Do not use strange words or abbreviated phrases. The better and clearer you explain yourself, the better the answer. This skill, writing good questions, is so important that we will dedicate an entire level to it later. Today we only practise.

When the chatbot answers, read it calmly. If it does not convince you, you can ask again: "explain it to me more simply", "give me another example", "shorter". The conversation is continuous, like a WhatsApp chat: you can add messages and the chatbot remembers what you said before in that same conversation. It is like telling a story to a friend over several messages.

A practical tip: you can change the language of the answer by simply asking in your language. If you write in Spanish, Portuguese, Catalan or English, the chatbot answers you in that same language. And if you want it in another language, ask: "answer me in Catalan". It also helps to practice languages, a bonus that costs nothing.

Be careful with one thing: the chatbot has not been born knowing everything. It has learned from texts up to a certain date, and it can be wrong or invent things when it does not know the answer. It is what experts call "hallucinations", but in a simple way: sometimes it makes things up to look good. That is why you should always check important data, such as amounts, dates or medical advice, with an official source or a trusted person.

And the last idea, the one that calms the fear: you cannot break anything. You can write whatever you want in that white box. The chatbot does not judge you, does not get tired and does not share your questions (in the basic version, check the privacy settings). It is like talking to the mirror: the more things you say, the more you learn about yourself. We are here to practise.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The cake recipe**: you want to bake a cake for the grandchild's birthday. You open ChatGPT and ask: "Give me a simple sponge cake recipe with ingredients I have at home." The chatbot answers with ingredients and steps. You write down what you need and go shopping.
2. **The medical letter from the council**: you have to write a letter asking for an appointment. You ask Gemini: "Help me write a polite letter to the health centre requesting an appointment." You copy the answer, adjust the details and print it.
3. **Explaining a term to a child**: your nephew asks you what a "budget" is. You ask the chatbot: "Explain what a budget is as if I were eight years old." The answer arrives simple and clear, and you repeat it to the child with your words.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open your browser and go to chatgpt.com or gemini.google.com.
2. If it asks you to log in, use the account you created in the previous level. Do not be afraid of the page.
3. Find the white box that says "Write a message" and click on it.
4. Write: "What is artificial intelligence? Explain it as if I were your grandfather." Press enter.
5. Read the answer calmly. Does it convince you? Does it have words you do not understand?
6. Write a second message: "Now explain it more simply, with an example from the kitchen." Press enter again.
7. Ask something about your daily life: a recipe, the timetable of a service, a letter. You choose.
8. Ask it to answer you in another of the course languages: "Answer me in Catalan."
9. End the day writing in your notebook: one thing the chatbot did well, and one thing you would improve.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
Everything you write to a chatbot is information. Do not give it personal secrets, passwords, bank details or health data that you would not put in a public poster. In the basic versions, your conversations are used to improve the models; read the privacy policy and use the available settings to protect yourself. And remember: the chatbot can lie to you politely. Important information always passes the filter of an official source and your common sense.
