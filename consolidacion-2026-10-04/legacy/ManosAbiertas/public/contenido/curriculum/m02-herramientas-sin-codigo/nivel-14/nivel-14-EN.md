# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 14
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Stem
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Review everything learned in the Stem band: Colab, spreadsheets, NotebookLM and presentations.
- Consolidate the habit of "ask, check, decide".
- Recognise when each tool is useful in daily life.
- Prepare the step towards the tools of the Branch band.
- Build a small "daily toolkit" with the four tools of the band.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Review | Looking again at what we have learned to fix it, like watering the garden again. |
| Toolkit | The set of tools you already know how to use, like the toolbox of the house. |
| Ask | Give clear instructions to the AI, with topic, context and objective. |
| Check | Review what the AI answers, read the numbers, confirm the data. |
| Decide | The last step and the most important: you choose, the AI only helps. |
| Band | Each stage of our tree: Seed, Root, Stem, Branch, Crown, Fruit. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Today we stop to review, and stopping is also part of learning. We have climbed a good part of our tree: the Root taught us to ask, and the Stem has taught us to use tools that do things. Before going up to the Branch, let us sit on the path, look at the ground we have covered, and fix what we have learned. A garden is not watered once; the water returns.

Let us remember the Stem band. Level 10 took us to Google Colab, where we saw that a notebook can contain text and parts that run tasks. Level 11 taught us spreadsheets: a table that calculates by itself, with AI at hand. Level 12 gave us NotebookLM, the secretary who has read all our documents. Level 13 taught us to present ideas with AI, with clear slides and your voice.

Four tools, one habit: ask, check, decide. We have repeated it in every level because it is the key. You ask the AI with a good question, with context. You check what it answers, because the AI can make mistakes or invent. And you decide, because the last word is always yours. Whoever has this habit has everything; whoever only has tools has nothing.

Let us ask ourselves when each tool is useful. Colab, when you want to experiment with something already prepared, without installing anything. Spreadsheets, for lists that calculate: budget, medicines, quotas. NotebookLM, when you have documents to understand: contracts, reports, letters. Presentations, when you have to explain something to others: the meeting, the school, the association.

Do we remember the metaphors? The spreadsheet is the wardrobe with shelves. NotebookLM is the attentive secretary. The presentation is the well-ordered notebook. The prompt is the good question we asked a specialist. The metaphors help us remember: our memory holds on to images better than to words.

Now, the most important review: the habit of asking well. The five golden rules of level 9 return: be concrete, give context, give examples, set limits, iterate. They serve for the chat, and they also serve for Colab, for the spreadsheet, for NotebookLM and for the presentations. The good question is a universal tool, the first of all.

Let us also review the security habit. In the Stem band we have used more personal tools: spreadsheets with our data, documents with our papers, presentations with our ideas. Everything that is personal deserves care: passwords, privacy settings, checking what we share. Security is not fear; it is order.

And let us review the confidence. At the beginning of this journey, many of you thought that this was impossible, that computers were not for you. Look where you have arrived: you have created a notebook, a table, a presentation. The proof is in what you have done. And there is still a long way to go up the tree.

Before moving on to the Branch, let us build our daily toolkit. Four tools, one habit: Colab, spreadsheet, NotebookLM, presentations, and the habit of asking, checking and deciding. Write it in your notebook: it is your map for the rest of the journey. When you face a task, you will know which tool to reach for.

The Branch band is next, and it is exciting: tools that do real things, that connect with the world, that let you try the AI of others without programming. But first, a practical exercise: let us build a small real project with the four tools of the Stem, from the idea to the presentation. It is the best way to fix what we have learned.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The project of the "grandparents' table"**: the community centre organises a monthly meal for the older people of the neighbourhood. You want to propose the idea to the committee. You use the spreadsheet for the budget, NotebookLM to read the centre's rules, and a presentation for the meeting.
2. **The health file**: you organise in a spreadsheet the medicines and appointments, and in NotebookLM you upload the reports. At the doctor, you arrive with everything clear: the table ordered and the questions prepared.
3. **The school activity**: you use Colab to try a prepared example about numbers, you summarise with NotebookLM the school rules, and you present the activity to the families with a clear presentation.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Take your notebook and write four headings: Colab, Spreadsheet, NotebookLM, Presentations.
2. Under each heading, write the answer: what is it for and when do I use it? Do not look at anything, use your memory.
3. Now choose a real idea of yours: a proposal, a project, a small task of your life.
4. Apply the toolkit: which tool fits each part? For example, the spreadsheet for the numbers, the presentation for the proposal.
5. Do a small round trip with each tool: open it, make a small test, close it. Warm-up like before a walk.
6. Write the habit in capital letters in your notebook: ASK, CHECK, DECIDE.
7. Read it out loud. This is what you take to the Branch.
8. Check with a colleague or in the forum: what has been the most difficult for you, and what is the clearest?
9. Close the notebook. You have finished the Stem. The Branch awaits you.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What are the four tools of the Stem band?
2. What is the habit that repeats in all levels?
3. Which tool would you use to understand a long contract?
4. Which tool would you use to calculate the budget of a party?
5. Why is "decide" the most important step?

**Answers:** 1. Colab, spreadsheets, NotebookLM and presentations. 2. Ask, check, decide. 3. NotebookLM: it reads it, summarises it and answers your questions. 4. The spreadsheet, which calculates by itself. 5. Because the AI helps, but the last word and the responsibility are yours.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
At the end of a band it is time to take stock, and taking stock also includes the ethical dimension. We have learned to do more things, and doing more things means more responsibility. The tools of the Stem handle personal data: budgets, documents, ideas. Treat them as you would treat someone else's belongings. And remember: the AI does not decide, you do. Whoever has a good tool and a good conscience, has everything.
