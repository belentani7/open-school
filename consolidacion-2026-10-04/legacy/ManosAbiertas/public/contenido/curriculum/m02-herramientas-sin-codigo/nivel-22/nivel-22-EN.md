## 📖 Vocabulario esencial
| Término | Explicación en palabras simples |
|---|---|
| Personal automation | A sequence of actions that runs by itself for you, without you being there. |
| Trigger | What starts the automation: a time, an email, a change in a file. |
| Action | What the automation does: send, copy, create, notify. |
| Filter | A condition that decides if the automation continues or stops. |
| Log | The history of what the automation has done, to review it. |
| Trust | Knowing that your automation works because you have tested it many times. |

## 📚 Lección principal
You already have your assistant (level 20) and you already know how to connect tools (level 21). Now comes the reward: the personal automation. An automation is like an invisible helper that does the boring work while you do the important things. It is not magic: it is a recipe that you write and the machine follows.

Every automation stands on three legs. First: the trigger. Something happens: it is eight in the morning, an email arrives with the word "invoice", you upload a photo to the family folder. Second: the filter (optional). Does it meet the conditions? Only invoices over 50 euros, only photos of the grandchildren, only working days. Third: the action. It runs: it saves the invoice in Drive, sends the alert to WhatsApp, creates the event in the calendar.

Let us look at real life. The "archived invoice": an email with the electricity bill arrives, the automation detects the PDF, saves it in the "Invoices 2024" folder in Drive and sends you an alert: "Electricity invoice saved". You do nothing: the email arrives, the automation works, you see the alert. Saving: 3 minutes per invoice, 36 a month.

The "birthday never forgotten": your database holds the birthdays. Every morning, the automation checks if there is one today. If there is, it sends you a message: "Today is your sister's birthday, call her". And if you want, it sends her an automatic greeting on WhatsApp with your name. Zero forgetfulness, zero last-minute rush. The same idea works for important dates such as renewing your NIE or the padrón.

The "voice of the agenda": every Sunday at eight, the automation reads your calendar for the week, makes a nice summary, and the voice (Piper) reads it to you while you have breakfast. "Monday: doctor at 10, shopping at 12. Tuesday: renewal appointment at the office at 4". You start the week knowing what is coming, without opening the calendar.

The "backup of what you write": every time you save a document in your "Papers" folder, the automation makes a copy in another cloud. If one day you delete it by accident, you have the copy. It is the insurance of your digital memory: the renewals, the contracts, the receipts.

The key to making it work: the patient test. Do not expect it to be perfect the first time. Build the automation, run it by hand, look at the log, fix the filter, test again. When you have tested it ten times and it has worked ten times, then you trust. Trust is earned with tests, not with faith.

And a golden rule: automate what you already know how to do by hand. If you do not know how to file an invoice, do not automate it. First learn the manual process, then automate it. The automation amplifies what you know; it does not fix what you do not understand.

Your time is the most valuable thing you have. Every minute that an automation saves you in the mechanical is a minute for your grandchildren, your garden, your reading, your rest. The Crown gives you the crown of time: use it.

In the next level we will take the automation to work: flows without programming, with IFTTT, Make and Zapier, so that your hours belong to you again.

## 💡 Ejemplos prácticos
1. **Archived invoice:** email with PDF → saved in Drive → alert on your mobile. 3 minutes per invoice saved.
2. **Birthdays:** database → every morning it checks → alert + automatic greeting. Zero forgetfulness.
3. **Agenda read aloud:** Sunday at 8 → calendar → summary → the voice reads it to you at breakfast.

## 🛠️ Actividad guiada
Paso 1. Identify three tasks that you do by hand and that repeat (filing, remembering, alerting, copying).
Paso 2. Choose one and write it in three steps: trigger, filter (if any), action.
Paso 3. Open Make, Zapier or IFTTT (whichever you use) and create the automation step by step.
Paso 4. Run it by hand (the "Run once" or "Test" button). Look at the log: did it work?
Paso 5. If it failed, adjust: did the trigger not catch the email? Was the filter too strict?
Paso 6. Test again. Repeat until it works 5 times in a row.
Paso 7. Activate it and let it run for a week. Check the log every day.
Paso 8. If everything goes well, create the second automation. One by one, your day is freed.

## ⚖️ Dimensión ética
- Automating is giving a machine permission to act in your name: that permission carries responsibility.
- Review what the automation does: do not use it to spy or to bother.
- Always keep a way to stop it: an automation that fails and sends a hundred messages is an annoyance that you created.
- Protect your personal data: automations that move bills and documents carry private information.
- Prudence is the brake of automation.
