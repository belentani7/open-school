# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 19
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Branch
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Review everything learned in the Branch band: Spaces, automations, databases and voice.
- Consolidate the Branch habit: try, test and choose.
- Recognise the power of open tools created by people who share.
- Prepare the step towards the Crown band, where you will create your own assistants.
- Build the complete toolkit: Seed, Root, Stem and Branch.

## 📚 Main lesson
We have reached the end of the Branch, and the tree is already big. In the Branch we discovered the most beautiful thing: a world of people who share. Let us stop and review, but not to repeat: to see how far we have come. Our tree now has leaves: the Root taught us to ask, the Stem gave us tools that do things, and the Branch opened the door to the work of others.

Let us remember the Branch band. Level 15 took us to Hugging Face: a great free library where people share models and Spaces. Level 16 taught us automation: a helper who never sleeps, who does the repeated tasks. Level 17 gave us the databases: the well-sorted warehouse that answers questions. Level 18, the voice: you speak and it writes, you give it a text and it reads. Four levels, four doors.

The great lesson of the Branch is the habit of trying. We discovered it in level 15 and we have used it all the way: not everything works the first time, and that is fine. You open, you try, you test, and if it does not work, you choose another. There are thousands. This habit is called exploration, and it is the engine of everything: whoever tries, learns; whoever does not try, stays at the door.

And the other great lesson: openness. The tools of the Branch are mostly open, created by people who share. We have learned to use them, but also to value them. Behind each Space, each model, each voice, there is a person who dedicated time. The ethical dimension of this band is gratitude: when you use the work of others, you use it with respect, and you share in your turn when you can.

Let us put the toolkit together. Look at what you have learned in this whole module: the Seed taught you to ask and to be safe. The Root taught you to write, to translate, to create images and to prompt well. The Stem gave you Colab, spreadsheets, NotebookLM and presentations. And the Branch has given you the Spaces, automations, databases and voice. The tree is complete, and it is yours.

Now, the review exercise: a real project that uses everything. Imagine you want to create a "neighbours' hour" in your community centre. The database organises the neighbours who join. The automation reminds them each month. NotebookLM summarises the centre's rules. The presentation explains the idea to the committee. And the voice records the minutes. Each tool in its place, and you, in the centre.

Let us also review the habit that accompanies everything: ask, check, decide. We learned it in the Stem and it serves in the Branch: you ask with a good question, you check what the machine answers, you decide. The Branch adds one more: try. Ask, check, decide and try. Four words that are worth a fortune.

And let us review the security. In the Branch we have handled more powerful tools: data of people, voice, automations that act alone. The more power, the more care. Check what you connect, protect what is personal, do not share what should not be shared. The tree is beautiful, but its roots are security and respect.

The Crown band is next, and it is the crown of our tree: you will create your own assistants. Not to program: to configure. You will choose the tools, you will connect them, you will make them work for you. The Branch taught you to use what others create; the Crown will teach you to create your own. Everything you have learned serves that.

Before going up, the confidence. Remember level 00? Many of you had never opened an application without fear. Look at you now: you know how to ask, how to create images, how to make a table calculate, how to understand a document, how to automate, how to store, how to speak to the machine. You are not the same people who started. The tree has grown, and you with it.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The "neighbours' hour"**: the project of the band: the database with the participants, the automation with the monthly reminder, the presentation for the committee. You build it with everything you have learned and you present it. The neighbours thank you, and the tree of the community grows.
2. **The group of caregivers**: a neighbour group that takes care of the elderly wants to organise itself. The database lists the people and the days; the automation reminds each one; the voice reads the announcements for those who do not read well. Technology at the service of care.
3. **The celebration of the neighbourhood**: a party for the street. The presentation proposes it to the association, the spreadsheet calculates the budget, the automation sends the invitations, and the voice records the thanks. A party organised by one person and many tools.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Take your notebook and look at the headings of the last four levels: Spaces, Automations, Databases, Voice.
2. Under each heading write, from memory, the tool you learned and one use in your life.
3. Now think of a real need of your community: a group, an event, a problem.
4. Divide it into parts: what needs data, what needs repetition, what needs to be explained, what needs the voice.
5. Assign a tool to each part. Look: you already have a plan.
6. Write the four words in capital letters: ASK, CHECK, DECIDE, TRY.
7. Read the whole notebook slowly: it is the map of your tree.
8. Share with a colleague: which level has been the most difficult, and which the most beautiful?
9. Close the notebook and look at the horizon: the Crown awaits. You are ready.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
The Branch has given you power, and power demands a good conscience. The open tools are a gift from people who share: do not abuse them, do not use them to deceive or harm. The data of the Branch is often personal: protect it, do not share it without permission. And the automations act alone: check what they do before they do it. The tree grows upwards, but it stands on its roots: respect and honesty. Without them, no crown is worth it.
