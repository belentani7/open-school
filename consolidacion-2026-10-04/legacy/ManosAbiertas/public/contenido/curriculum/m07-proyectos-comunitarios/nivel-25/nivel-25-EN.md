# Module 7: Community Projects — Level 25
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Fruit
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Learn to close the circle of a community project with gratitude.
- Celebrate the road travelled, the achievements and the people.
- Give thanks for what has been achieved, together.
- Say goodbye well to what ends and open what begins.
- Conclude module 7 and recognise the complete journey.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Celebration | Stopping, looking and celebrating the road travelled together. |
| Gratitude | Recognising what we have received from others and from the community. |
| Cycle | The complete time of a project: beginning, growth and closing. |
| Closing | The conscious and dignified end of a stage. |
| New beginning | Opening a new stage with what has been learned and thanked. |
| Harvest | The fruit of everything that was planted and cared for. |

## 📚 Main lesson
We have reached the end of module 7: level 25. Levels like the steps of a ladder, seeds that became roots, trunk, branches, canopy and fruit. This last level is the celebration: stopping, looking at the road travelled and giving thanks. The celebration is not a full stop: it is a place of passage.

A community project lives in cycles: it begins, grows, gives fruit and closes, so another one can begin. Closing a cycle is not failing: it is completing. Like the season that ends so another one arrives. Those who know how to close well, know how to begin well; those who do not close, accumulate and get tired.

The closing is conscious and dignified. A project is not abandoned like a house left in a hurry: it is closed with care, thanking those who took part, keeping what was learned and leaving the door open. The dignified closing honours what was done and prepares what comes.

The celebration belongs to everyone. It is not a party only for the leaders: it is for every hand that planted, every voice that spoke, every person who arrived early. At the celebration, nobody is left out: the whole community looks at what it built and recognises itself in it.

Gratitude is the heart of the celebration. Giving thanks for what has been achieved: the harvest, the friendship, the trust that was formed. Gratitude is not formality: it is truth. When we say "thank you" with truth, what was done gains its just value.

Giving thanks is also looking back without sadness. There were difficulties, mistakes and tiredness; there were also learning, achievements and joys. Gratitude does not hide the difficult: it thanks what the difficult taught. Every obstacle crossed is also a step taken.

At the celebration, we tell the story. The community meets and remembers: how it began, who helped, what happened along the road. Telling the story is keeping it: those who hear it feel part of it; those who tell it see its value. Shared stories are the treasure of the community.

The celebration recognises the people. Every participant is seen: those who arrived first, those who arrived later, those who stayed until the end. A gesture, a word, a small gift made by hand: simple ways of saying "I saw you, your work matters". Nobody works only for themselves.

The closing leaves the door open. What ends does not erase what remains: the knowledge, the trust, the bonds. The project can end and the community continues. From what we close with dignity, what we will open is born: the new beginning.

The new beginning does not have to be grand. It can be a new idea, a new group, a new cycle of the same project. What matters is that it is born from what was learned and thanked: nothing was lost, everything was transformed. Every end is a beginning waiting.

This level is also the harvest of the whole module. From level 00 to 24, we planted the seed, got to know the community, learned the values of the open hands, built the team, decided together, solved conflicts, communicated, led, planned, evaluated. All of this is what we now celebrate.

Let us remember: the open hands. This is the symbol of the module: not closed hands that keep, but open hands that give and receive. At the celebration, the hands open: to give thanks, to embrace, to hand over what was learned and receive what the community gives back.

When we finish this level, we finish module 7. But the community does not finish: it continues, with what it learned, making new projects grow. May this celebration be a place of passage: here a cycle closes with gratitude, and from here we set out towards the next one, with hope.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The closing party.** The community meets to close the circle of the seed bank. They tell the story of the beginning, thank the founders and those who joined, and share a meal made from the gardens. It ends in celebration, not in silence.
2. **The recognition of everyone.** At the closing, each person receives a small seed with a written word: "you planted", "you watered", "you cared", "you taught". A simple gift that says "I saw you, you are part of this".
3. **The open door.** When closing a cycle, the community writes down what it learned and who wants to continue. A new group is born, for the same neighbourhood, with the same spirit: the end of one cycle was the beginning of the next.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Mark the date of the closing celebration, with the whole community.
Step 2: Prepare the story: who remembers the beginning, the road and the achievements?
Step 3: Give thanks: to each person, a word or a gesture of gratitude.
Step 4: Recognise everyone: nobody is left out of the celebration.
Step 5: Remember what was learned: what did the whole module leave in the community?
Step 6: Close with dignity: what is kept, what is thanked, what is left?
Step 7: Open the new beginning: what does the community want to start next?

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is closing a cycle? a) Failing. b) Completing a stage with consciousness and dignity. c) Abandoning in a hurry.
2. What is the heart of the celebration? a) The food. b) Gratitude. c) The speeches.
3. What is telling the story for? a) To show off. b) To keep and share the value of the road. c) To waste time.
4. What does the dignified closing do? a) It leaves the door open. b) It closes everything forever. c) It forgets the past.
5. What is the new beginning? a) An end. b) A new stage born from what was learned and thanked. c) Nothing.

Answers: 1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-a, 5-b.
