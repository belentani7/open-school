# Module 8: Ethics, Future and Governance — Level 19
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Branch
## Estimated time: 2 hours

- Review the whole Branch band: work, rights, identity and cybersecurity.
- Join the branches into a single trunk of digital confidence.
- Review the personal defence plan and update it.

- Prepare for the Canopy band: AI governance.

## 📚 Main lesson
We finish the Branch band. In the tree's branches, the plant opens to the world: it gives shade, bears fruit and resists the winds. That is how it is for us: we already have branches of knowledge about work, rights, identity and security. Now it is time to review, join and update the plan we have been building since the Seed band.

Let us review the branches. In level 15 we saw that AI changes work: it takes on repetitive tasks, leaves the human ones to people and creates new trades. Experience and dealing with people are our treasure. In level 16 we learned the digital rights: access, learning, protection, equality and participation. Learning at any age is a right. In level 17 we took care of our identity and reputation: thinking before publishing and asking permission for photos. In level 18 we assembled the cybersecurity kit: the three "no"s, the double-check and the call back.

If we join all those branches, a trunk comes out: digital confidence. Confidence is not naivety, it is competence. It is knowing what we do, why we do it and what to do if something goes wrong. A person with digital confidence is not afraid of the internet, but neither do they walk with their eyes covered. They walk with open eyes and a hand on the railing, like someone going down familiar stairs.

Now comes the assessment. Digital health can be looked at the way we look at body health: without alarmism, with honesty. Is my phone locked? Are my important passwords strong? Do I have backups of what I want? Do I know how to handle suspicious messages? Do I know how to ask for explanations when something does not add up? Each sincere answer is a thermometer of our digital health.

Self-assessment is not to judge ourselves, but to grow. Nobody is born knowing. If there is a branch we have that is weaker, no problem: it is reinforced. It is like the garden: if a plant does not bear fruit, we do not pull it out, we fertilise it. Digital maturity is built with small continuous improvements, not with one-day perfections.

Our personal plan deserves an update. In level 04 we made a first plan; in level 09 we turned it into a defence plan. Now, with what we learned in the Branch band, we complete it: adding the review of our reputation, the care of photos, the call back, the assessment of our digital health. The plan grows with us, like the tree grows with its branches.

It is also time to recognise how much we have advanced. At the beginning of the module, ethics and AI sounded like distant things to us. Now we talk about biases, laws, rights and scams with ease. That progress is real and deserves pride. Learning at our age is not a step backwards: it is a victory. And every level passed is one more branch.

The Branch band leaves us ready for the Canopy. In the Canopy, the highest part of the tree, we will see things from above: how organisations, companies and institutions govern artificial intelligence. It will no longer be just our house and our street: it will be the square and the town hall. And we, citizens of that square, will have a lot to say.

Before climbing to the Canopy, let us close the band with an image. The tree of our digital security has roots (the basic habits), a stem (the laws that protect us) and branches (work, rights, identity, security). All of that we have built together, level by level. Now, the tree can face the wind: we are ready for the heights.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **Rosa's thermometer.** Rosa does her self-assessment: she has her phone locked, strong passwords and a backup. She detects that she still falls for messages with "prizes". She notes it down and reinforces that branch.
2. **Antonio's review.** Antonio updates his defence plan adding the call-back rule and the once-a-year review of his reputation.
3. **Carmen's victory.** Carmen looks back and remembers that at first she did not know what a strong password was. Now she explains to her sister how to protect herself from scams. That is the fruit of learning.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Take your defence plan (you updated it in level 09) and read it completely.
Step 2: Write in your notebook: "Assessment of my digital health" and do the 5-question test: phone locked? strong passwords? backups? do I know how to detect scams? do I know how to ask for explanations?
Step 3: Mark with a ✓ the answers that are yes and with a ✗ those that are not yet.
Step 4: Choose the weakest branch (just one) and write a concrete action to reinforce it this week.
Step 5: Add a new section to the plan: "Care of my reputation" with two rules (think before publishing, ask permission for photos).
Step 6: Ask an AI chat: "What digital security habits are most important for a 60-year-old?". Read it and note whether anything is missing from your plan.
Step 7: Reflect aloud: what have you learned in the Branch band that makes you feel safer?
