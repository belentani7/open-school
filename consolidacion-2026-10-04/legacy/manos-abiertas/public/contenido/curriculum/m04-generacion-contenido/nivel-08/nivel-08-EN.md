# Module 4: Content Generation — Level 08
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Root
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Choose and control the style of AI-generated images.
- Refine an image: correct details and ask for specific changes.
- Know the names of the most common styles (photo, painting, drawing, comic).
- Use aspect ratio and composition in the description.
- Get images that look more like what we imagine.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Style | The visual technique: photography, watercolour, oil, comic, children's drawing. |
| Refine | Adjust the generated image to improve it. Like polishing wood. |
| Aspect ratio | The shape of the image: square, vertical, panoramic. |
| Composition | How the elements are arranged inside the image. |
| Lighting | The light of the image: morning, night, studio. |
| Image prompt | The detailed description you give to the image generator. |

## 📚 Main lesson
In level 03 we learned how to create images with a description. In this level we go one step further: we will learn to control the style, correct mistakes and refine the result until it looks like what we have in our head.

The style is the "painting technique" of the image. If we say "realistic photography", the AI tries to imitate a photo. If we say "oil painting" or "watercolour", it imitates those techniques. If we say "comic drawing" or "children's drawing", the result changes completely. Style is the clothing of the content.

To describe style well, we must use words the AI understands. Some very useful ones: "realistic photography", "oil painting", "watercolour", "pencil drawing", "comic", "caricature", "old poster style", "anime style". The more style words we know, the more control we have.

Refining is the art of correcting. The first image may have flaws: a strange hand, badly written text, a wrong colour. We can tell the AI "repeat it without the text on the wall", "change the colour of the dress to blue", "add more flowers". Each change is an improvement.

Some generators allow you to edit only part of the image, pointing at the area you want to change. This is very useful: if you do not like the background but you like the subject, you change only the background. It is like retouching a photo with a brush.

The aspect ratio can also be requested: "square image", "vertical image" or "panoramic image". Depending on where you will use the image (a poster, a card, a banner), one shape or another suits better. The shape is decided before creating.

Composition is how we arrange things. We can ask "the cat in the centre", "the house in the background", "the first person with hand extended". The AI understands these instructions, although not always the first time. The more concrete we are, the better.

Lighting changes the mood: "morning light", "warm home light", "neon light", "sunset light". The same scene with different light gives different feelings. It is like the time of day in a real photograph.

A refined image prompt is built in layers: the subject, the setting, the style, the lighting and the shape. For example: "an orange cat" (subject) "in an old kitchen" (setting) "realistic photography" (style) "warm afternoon light" (lighting) "square image" (shape).

Patience is key. Sometimes it takes three or four attempts to get the right image. Each attempt teaches something: which words work and which do not. It is like learning to make an omelette: the first day it comes out average, the tenth, perfect.

We can also reuse the prompt that worked. If we get an image we love, we save the description in a document. This way we have our own "recipe box" of images to reuse in the future.

A warning: do not expect technical perfection. Hands, fingers and small text remain difficult for AI. If the flaw is small, we can fix it with simple editing tools or simply regenerate.

By the end of this level, we will know how to direct style like a photography director: choosing the light, the shape and the technique, and correcting until the image tells what we want.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The birthday card.** Marta wants an elegant card. She asks for "a bouquet of wild flowers, watercolour painting, soft morning light, square image". She refines twice until the colour fits.
2. **The club poster.** Pedro makes a poster for the reading club: "a stack of books with a cup of coffee, realistic photography, warm afternoon light". He asks for a vertical version for the street pole.
3. **The family portrait.** Ana generates "a portrait of the grandparents sitting on the porch, pencil drawing, vertical image". She corrects the grandfather's face by regenerating until it resembles him.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Open Bing Image Creator (bing.com/images/create).
Step 2: Write this prompt: "a village house with pots of geraniums on the balcony, realistic photography, sunset light, square image".
Step 3: Press create and wait. Look at the result.
Step 4: Write a refined version: "the same house, now in oil painting style, and with a cat at the door".
Step 5: If there is any flaw, ask for a specific change: "make the door brown wood".
Step 6: Try changing the aspect ratio: "vertical version of this image".
Step 7: When you have the image you like, save it to the gallery.
Step 8: Write the prompt that worked best in a document (your image "recipe book") to use another day.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is the style of an image? a) The visual technique: photo, painting, comic. b) The file size. c) The price.
2. If the first image has a flaw, what do you do? a) Give up. b) Ask for a specific change. c) Delete the program.
3. What is the aspect ratio? a) The shape of the image: square, vertical, panoramic. b) The background colour. c) The number of colours.
4. Can you change only part of the image? a) No, never. b) Yes, some generators allow it. c) Only with money.
5. What is the key to improving images? a) Rushing. b) Patience and attempts. c) Not trying.

Answers: 1-a, 2-b, 3-a, 4-b, 5-b.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- Do not generate images of real people without their consent, especially in false situations.
- If the image imitates the work of a living artist, be cautious: style is not property, but respect is.
- Do not use generated images to impersonate, deceive or scam.
- Check the generator's terms before using the images for commercial purposes.
- Be transparent when an image you share was created by AI.
