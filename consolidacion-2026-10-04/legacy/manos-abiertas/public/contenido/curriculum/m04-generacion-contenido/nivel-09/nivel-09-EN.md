# Module 4: Content Generation — Level 09
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Root
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Create automatic presentations with AI (Gamma, Canva).
- Prepare the content you want to tell before creating the slides.
- Choose a design and let the AI organise it.
- Review and correct the presentation so it tells your story well.
- Present with confidence using clear and simple slides.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Presentation | A set of slides projected to explain a topic. |
| Slide | Each page of the presentation. Like each page of a notebook. |
| Template | A ready-made design you can fill with your content. |
| Outline | The script with the main ideas in order. |
| Title | The main sentence of each slide. |
| Present | Explain the topic to an audience using the slides. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Have we ever had to explain something to a group? A talk at the association, the presentation of a club project, a class for the crafts group. Presentations are the perfect tool, and now AI can make almost all of them for us.

A presentation is not a document: it is a story in images and short sentences. Each slide says one idea, and the ideas follow one another like the chapters of a book. The golden rule: little text and lots of meaning. If the slide has too many words, nobody will read it.

Before asking anything from the AI, it is good to be clear about what we want to tell. Like when you prepare a meal: first you decide the menu and then you cook. We can prepare three or four main ideas. With that, the AI will have enough to work with.

AI presentation tools work like this: you write the topic, choose a design and the program generates the slides with text and images. The best known are Gamma and Canva presentations. Both have a free version.

In Gamma, the process is very simple: you give it the topic, choose a design style and the approximate number of slides, and it creates the complete presentation. Afterwards you can change texts, colours and images to your taste.

In Canva, there are also ready-made design templates and an image library. AI can help us write the text of each slide or find the right image. It is like getting a decorator and a copywriter for free.

The outline is the heart of the presentation. Before generating, we can ask the AI: "Give me a 5-slide outline about the benefits of an urban vegetable garden". We review the outline and, if we like it, we tell it to create the presentation. This way we control the story from the start.

Each slide must have a clear title and few lines. If the AI puts too much text, we simplify it: we cut sentences, remove repetitions and leave only the essentials. Remember: people read the slides while they listen to you; they must not have two tasks at once.

Images help understanding. We can use our own photos or AI-generated images (remembering what we learned in previous levels). A picture is worth a thousand words, but only if it relates to what we are saying.

Before presenting, you always rehearse. We read the slides out loud, check that the order makes sense and that there are no mistakes. If something does not fit, we fix it. Rehearsal turns a good presentation into a great one.

During the talk, the slides are our support, not our script. We do not read the screen: we look at the people and use the slides to remind ourselves of the thread. Confidence is gained through preparation, and preparation includes knowing well what is on each screen.

A final tip: less is more. Five clear slides are worth more than twenty full ones. AI helps us create quickly, but we bring the clarity through review. The presentation is yours even if the machine assembled it.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The association talk.** Carmen prepares a talk about recycling. She writes the topic in Gamma, chooses a green design and lets the AI create 8 slides. Then she adds photos of the bins in her neighbourhood.
2. **The club project.** Pedro presents the urban vegetable garden project to the town council. He uses Canva, asks the AI for a 6-slide outline and reviews it with his granddaughter before the meeting.
3. **The cooking class.** Marta teaches how to make pasties at the social centre. She creates a simple presentation with photos of each step and little text, so the students can follow without getting lost.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
Step 1: Open the browser and type "gamma.app". Create a free account with your email.
Step 2: Press "New presentation" or the equivalent button.
Step 3: In the topic box write: "How to make tomato preserves at home, for beginners".
Step 4: Choose a design you like from the ones offered (click on it) and press "Generate".
Step 5: Wait a few seconds. The AI will create the presentation with several slides.
Step 6: Review each slide. Click on the texts and correct them: remove what is extra, add your own tips.
Step 7: Change an image if you are not happy with it: look for a photo of tomatoes in the image library.
Step 8: Press "Present" or "View" to see it full screen. If you are happy, save it and rehearse by reading it out loud.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
- If the presentation contains data (figures, dates), check them before presenting.
- Do not use other people's photos or texts without permission. Remember what you learned about copyright.
- Do not put personal data of attendees or third parties in the presentation.
- Be honest: if the images are AI-generated, you can say so if asked.
- The content is your responsibility: check there are no mistakes before the audience sees it.
