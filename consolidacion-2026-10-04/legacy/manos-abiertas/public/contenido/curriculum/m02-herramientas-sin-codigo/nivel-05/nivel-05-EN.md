# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 05
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Root
## Estimated time: 3 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Get to know ChatGPT in depth: its screen, its parts and its ways of working.
- Learn the difference between a new chat and continuing a conversation.
- Use conversations as organised folders for your topics.
- Discover the most useful tools of the interface: regenerate, copy, voice input.
- Build a daily routine with ChatGPT that saves you time.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Interface | Everything you see on the screen of the application: buttons, boxes and menus. |
| New chat | A conversation that starts from scratch, like a new notebook for a new topic. |
| History | The list of all your past conversations, like the titles in a diary. |
| Regenerate | A button that asks the chatbot to give you another version of its answer. |
| Copy | A button that takes the answer to the clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. |
| Voice input | Speaking instead of writing: the microphone hears you and writes what you say. |

## 📚 Main lesson
Now that we have planted the seed, it is time for the root: knowing the tool well. ChatGPT is the most famous assistant, and its screen hides everything you need for daily life. In this level we are going to tour it calmly, as we would a new house: room by room.

The first thing you see when you enter is a large empty space, the white box below where you write, and on the left a side list. That side list is your history: every conversation you have is saved there with a name. It is like the index of a recipe book: you do not read the whole book again to find a dish, you look at the index. That is what the history is for.

Here is a trick that changes everything: use a new chat for each topic. If you ask about the pension, then about a recipe, then about a trip, do it in three separate conversations. Why? Because the chatbot "remembers" only the current conversation. If you mix topics, it gets confused and the answers get muddled. A chat per topic is like a drawer per type of clothing: everything is easy to find.

To start a new chat there is usually a button with a small note or the words "New chat". It is the equivalent of opening a new notebook. Do not be afraid to have many: the history keeps them all, and you can return to any of them at any time by clicking on its title. Your topics are never lost.

Now, the chat box. Below, where you write, there is usually a little microphone icon. That is the voice input: you press it, speak, and the chatbot writes what you say. If writing costs you effort, this is your best friend. Practise it today, because it turns the phone into a dictation machine.

When the chatbot answers you, look at the bottom of the answer. There is usually a small button to copy: a symbol of two sheets. Pressing it takes the answer to the clipboard, which is like a temporary pocket where copied things are stored. Then you can paste it wherever you want: in a message, in a document, in an email. Paste is the same as putting what you had in your pocket on the table.

There is also a button to "regenerate". It means asking the chatbot "give me another try". If the answer did not convince you, press it and it will answer you again in another way. It is like asking the cook to prepare the dish again, with a different touch. Sometimes the second version is better than the first.

You can also mark an answer as good or bad, with a thumb up or down. That helps the chatbot learn what you like. It is like telling the waiter "this dish, make it like this for me from now on". It costs you one tap and makes the tool more yours.

ChatGPT has different versions: the free one is enough for daily life. The paid ones add more power, but a person of our age does not need them to start. Do not pay until you have squeezed the free version. Also know that the phone application and the web page are the same: use the one that is most comfortable for you.

A very useful habit: give the conversation a useful name. Many assistants let you rename each chat. If you call it "Granddaughter's birthday menu", you will find it later just by looking at the list. A good name in the history is like a good label on a jar in the pantry.

Finally, remember that ChatGPT also works with other languages and that you can ask it to write in the language you need. And above all, use it as a habit, not as a spectacle. Ask it the things of your life: the menu, the letter, the doubt, the idea. The more you use it, the more natural it becomes, like riding a bicycle.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The shopping list**: you create a new chat called "Weekly shopping". You ask: "Make me a shopping list for two people for a week, based on a Mediterranean diet, with a budget of forty euros." You copy the answer, print it or keep it open, and go shopping without forgetting anything.
2. **The visit to the grandchildren**: you create a chat "Ideas for the weekend". You ask: "Three plans to do with an eight-year-old grandson on Saturday, cheap and at home." You reformulate until you find the one you like, and you save the idea.
3. **The pill reminder**: you create a chat "My health". You ask: "Make me a simple table with the schedule to take my pills: morning, midday and night, with spaces to write." You copy it and put it on the fridge.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open ChatGPT on the web or on your phone.
2. Look at the interface: find the history on the left (or in the menu).
3. Press "New chat" and call it "My first organised chat".
4. Use the voice input: press the microphone and say "what can I ask you about?". See how it writes your words.
5. Ask a practical question: "Give me three ideas for dinner tonight with what is in the fridge."
6. If the answer is not convincing, press "regenerate" and compare.
7. Press the copy button and paste the answer into a note in your notebook.
8. Start another new chat with another topic, for example "Gifts for the family".
9. Write in your notebook the three buttons you have learned: new chat, copy and regenerate.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is the history in ChatGPT for?
2. Why is it a good idea to use a separate chat for each topic?
3. What does the copy button do and what is the clipboard?
4. What does "regenerate" mean and when is it useful?
5. How does voice input help you?

**Answers:** 1. To keep all your conversations and return to them whenever you want, like the index of a book. 2. Because the chatbot only remembers the current conversation; separating topics avoids confusing answers. 3. It takes the answer to the clipboard, a temporary pocket, to paste it elsewhere. 4. Ask the chatbot to give you another version of the answer; useful when it did not convince you. 5. It lets you speak instead of writing; the microphone writes what you say, ideal if writing costs you effort.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
ChatGPT keeps your conversations. That history is personal information: a doctor's question, a money concern, a family issue. Do not write in the chat anything you would not put on a public poster. Also, when you copy an answer, remember that it is not yours: it can be used, but verify it and, if it is for work or publication, mention that it was made with the help of AI. Transparency builds trust.

## 🧠 Summary and bridge
ChatGPT is a tool with a home: history organises your topics, the microphone lets you speak, copy stores the answers, and regenerate gives you second chances. Use a chat per topic and you will have everything in order. In the next level we will learn to use writing assistants, turning a simple idea into a well-written letter, article or message for any occasion.
