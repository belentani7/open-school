# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 12
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Stem
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what NotebookLM is and what it can do with your documents.
- Learn to upload documents and ask questions about them.
- Use the AI to summarise long texts in a few minutes.
- Create notes and study materials from a document.
- Put order in the paperwork of life: contracts, receipts, letters.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Notebook | In NotebookLM it is a workspace where you put your documents and notes together. |
| Source | Each document you add: a text, a PDF, a web page. The material the AI will work with. |
| Summary | A short version of a long text, with the most important ideas. |
| Question | You ask the AI about your documents, and it answers only with what is in them. |
| Note | A small text that the AI prepares for you to take away, like an index card. |
| Grounded answer | An answer that is based only on your documents, not on what the AI invented. |

## 📚 Main lesson
In our lives we accumulate papers: contracts, receipts, letters, regulations, reports. Reading them all takes hours, and understanding them even more. Today we learn a tool that was designed for exactly that: NotebookLM, a free Google tool that reads your documents and answers your questions about them. It is like a very attentive secretary who has read everything and remembers everything.

Let us understand the difference with the chatbots we already know. A chatbot answers from everything it learned on the internet. NotebookLM answers only from your documents: what you have uploaded. If it is not in your papers, it does not make it up. That is why its answers are "grounded", that is, based on something real. It is like asking a lawyer who has read your file, and only your file.

The name "Notebook" is not by chance. It works like a real notebook: you create one for each topic, add your documents, and alongside you make notes. Imagine you have a notebook for the flat: in it you put the contract, the receipts of the community, the insurance. Everything together, in one place, and you can ask questions: "When do I have to pay the community fee?" And it answers you from the receipts.

How do you use it? You enter notebooklm.google.com with your Google account. You create a notebook, you add sources: you can upload documents, PDFs, or paste a web page. In a few seconds the AI reads them. Then you have a box to ask questions, and it answers you based on what you have uploaded. It is that simple.

The most useful function is the summary. If you have a 20-page contract and you do not have time to read it, you upload it and ask: "Summarise this contract in ten lines." The AI does it for you, and it also offers you prepared notes: an index, a guide, key ideas. It is like having someone who has read the book for you and tells you the story well.

Another marvellous function: the questions with answers based on your document. You can ask "what is the deadline to give notice if I leave the flat?", and the answer includes the part of the document where it says it, with a note so you can check it. It does not invent, it points to the source. That trust is what makes it different from other tools.

Let us think about daily applications. The health: you upload the reports of your tests and you ask "what do the values of this analysis mean?" The answer will be based on the report and the words of the doctor. The house: the receipts, the insurance, the rules of the community, everything organised. The school: your daughter's documents, the schedules, the notices.

NotebookLM also allows you to create study materials. If you upload a chapter and ask for "a list of the ten key ideas", it prepares it for you. If you ask for "three questions to test what I have learned", it prepares them. It is a tutor that works with your materials, not with generic ones.

A golden rule: use NotebookLM for things that matter to you. The more personal the documents, the more valuable the tool. But remember, as always: it reads and summarises, but you decide. A summary is a help, not a substitute. For an important decision, a contract, a signature, always read the original or ask a trusted person.

And an advice for life: put order in your papers once and for all. Create a notebook for the house, another for health, another for the school. Little by little you will have everything organised and, above all, understood. The papers of a lifetime, finally explained.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The contract of the flat**: you upload the rental contract and ask: "Summarise it and tell me my obligations as a tenant." The AI answers with a summary and the three things you must do. You no longer need to read the 20 pages to know the essential.
2. **The medical report**: you upload the analysis report and ask: "Explain to me in simple words what these values mean." The answer is based on the report and uses simple language. You arrive at the doctor with questions already prepared.
3. **The community rules**: the community has sent you a long regulation. You upload it and ask: "What are the rules about noise and schedules?" You have the answer in a minute, without looking for hours.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open notebooklm.google.com with your Google account.
2. Look at the screen: find the button "New notebook" and create one called "My papers".
3. Add a source: you can upload a document you have, or use one of the examples that the tool offers.
4. Wait for the AI to read the document. Look at how it appears in the sources list.
5. Ask a question in the box: "Summarise this document in ten lines."
6. Read the summary and the notes that the tool prepares for you.
7. Ask a specific question: "What is the most important thing I should know?"
8. Look at the answer: does it include the part of the document it refers to?
9. Write in your notebook: what have you understood today that before would have taken you hours?

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is NotebookLM and what does it do with your documents?
2. What is the difference between a chatbot and NotebookLM?
3. What is a "source" and how do you add it?
4. Why are the answers of NotebookLM "grounded"?
5. What can you create in addition to summaries?

**Answers:** 1. A free Google tool that reads your documents and answers your questions based on them. 2. The chatbot answers from the internet; NotebookLM only from your documents. 3. Each document you add: text, PDF or web page, and you add it with the "add source" button. 4. Because it only uses what is in your documents and shows you the part it refers to. 5. Prepared notes: indexes, guides, key ideas and study questions.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
You are going to upload very personal documents: contracts, medical reports, private letters. That data is valuable and sensitive. Use the tool with your Google account and check the privacy settings; do not upload documents with other people's data without being sure. And remember the other side: NotebookLM can be used to "read" the papers of others. Never upload documents that are not yours without permission. Respect for privacy is the foundation of trust.
