# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 13
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Stem
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what a presentation is and why it is useful to share ideas.
- Learn to create slides with AI in a few minutes.
- Use the AI to build the outline and the visual style of the presentation.
- Adapt a presentation to a specific audience.
- Present with confidence, without being afraid of technology.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Presentation | A set of slides that support your explanation, like a well-ordered notebook. |
| Slide | Each page of the presentation: one idea, one screen. |
| Outline | The plan of the presentation: the chapters and their order, like the index of a book. |
| Template | A ready-made design that gives colour and style to the slides, so you do not start from zero. |
| Visual style | The set of colours, letters and images that give the presentation a uniform look. |
| Speaker | The person who presents, you: the AI is only the assistant. |

## 📚 Main lesson
We all have moments when we have to explain something: at the association, at the school, to a neighbour, at a community meeting. Today we learn how to create a presentation with AI in a few minutes, and above all, how to present without fear. Because the important thing is not the slides: it is you.

A presentation is a set of slides, each one a page with one idea. It is like a well-ordered notebook that supports your words. The screen does not speak alone: it accompanies you. Good slides are simple: a title, a few points, an image. The rest is in your voice.

The AI turns this task into a pleasure. You ask a tool like Gamma, Canva or Google Slides with Gemini: "Make me a presentation about the community rules, for a meeting with the neighbours." The AI creates the outline, the slides, the images and the style. You review it, you change what you want, and you present. It is like having an assistant designer and writer at your side.

The secret is the prompt, the instructions you give. The AI creates better slides when you tell it three things: the topic, who you are presenting to, and what you want to achieve. "A presentation about the garden at the community centre, for the neighbours, so that they sign up to help." More context, better result. We already know this from previous levels: the good question.

The outline is the soul of the presentation. Before the slides, the plan: what am I going to say, in what order? The AI proposes an outline and you check it. A good outline is like a good index: it lets you follow the thread. If the AI has forgotten something important, you add it. You are the boss, she is the assistant.

The style also matters, but it does not have to be complicated. The AI offers you templates: colour, letter, layout. Choose one that suits the occasion. For a community meeting, clear and calm. For a school party, colourful. Do not overload: slides with few words are read better. Less is more.

Now, the most valuable thing: presenting. Technology helps, but the courage is yours. When you have the slides ready, practise out loud three times. Speak slowly, look at the people, not at the screen. If you do not know something, say so. The audience is on your side: they want you to do well.

Let us look at a practical example. The community wants to improve the entrance gate. You create a presentation: the problem, the photos, the budget, the proposal. You ask the AI: "Organise these ideas into a presentation." It organises them. You present at the meeting. You no longer need to be a designer or an expert: you are a neighbour with a clear proposal.

Another daily use: the school. Your daughter's class asks for volunteers to explain an activity. Or you want to explain your work to the school. With AI, you prepare a presentation in twenty minutes. What before was a Sunday afternoon of work, now is a walk.

And an important advice: always check. The AI can invent data, a date, a number. Review every slide before presenting: read the numbers out loud, confirm the facts. A wrong date in front of the community is worse than not presenting. You are the one who guarantees the truth of what you say.

Let us also talk about the fear of technology. A presentation is just a file, and files do not bite. If you break something, you do it again. If you do not know how to change a slide, you ask the AI or a colleague. The important thing is the idea you have, and that is in your head, not in the screen.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The community meeting**: you have to propose the new watering system for the garden. You ask the AI for a presentation with the problem, the photos and the budget. The neighbours see it clearly and decide.
2. **The activity of the school**: your daughter's class asks you to explain a craft activity. You ask the AI: "A presentation to explain a paper craft to children, simple and with many pictures." In twenty minutes it is ready.
3. **The association of the neighbourhood**: you want to present the end-of-year summary. You ask the AI to organise your notes into slides with the most important data. You review the numbers and present with confidence.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open a presentation tool: for example Gamma (gamma.app) or Canva (canva.com).
2. Look at the screen: find the button to create a new presentation.
3. Write your prompt: a topic, an audience, an objective. For example: "A presentation about my neighbourhood, for new neighbours, so that they feel welcome."
4. Wait for the AI to create the slides. Observe the outline it proposes.
5. Review each slide: is there an idea per slide? Are the words few?
6. Change what you do not like: a title, a colour, an image. The AI is at your service.
7. Read the slides out loud, as if you were presenting. Check the data.
8. Ask the AI: "Make the text simpler" or "Use a calmer style." Try it.
9. Write in your notebook: what have you learned today that will help you in your life?

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
A presentation with AI can be used to inform, but also to convince. Use it to share clear ideas, not to dazzle with slides that hide the truth. Do not invent data to win the discussion: an invented number, when discovered, destroys all your credibility. And respect the images: the AI creates them, but if you use photos of people, ask for permission. In the community, as in life, what wins is not the best slides, but the best truth.

## 🔓 Open tools
- **Gamma** (gamma.app) — creates complete presentations from a single prompt, free.
- **Canva** (canva.com) — the design tool with templates and AI, free in its basic version.
- **Google Slides** (slides.google.com) — the free and simple classic, connected to Gemini.
- **LibreOffice Impress** (libreoffice.org) — the open-source presentation tool that lives on your computer.
- **SlidesAI** (slidesai.io) — an AI that creates Google Slides from a text.
