# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 15
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Branch
## Estimated time: 4 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand what Hugging Face is and what "Spaces" are.
- Learn to use ready-made AI tools created by other people.
- Try models of images, text and audio without programming.
- Recognise that behind each Space there is a person who created it.
- Discover the world of open AI, where everyone shares.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Hugging Face | A community on the internet where people share AI tools, like a great free library. |
| Space | A ready-made tool hosted there: you open it, use it, and you do not need to install anything. |
| Model | The "brain" of an AI: the thing that has learned and that produces the result. |
| Community | The people who create and share: researchers, teachers, curious people from all over the world. |
| Open | When something is open, everyone can see it, use it and improve it. |
| Free | It does not cost money to use, although some Spaces ask for a bit of patience. |

## 📚 Main lesson
We have reached the Branch, and the Branch changes things: until now we have used the tools of big companies, prepared for everyone. Today we discover that there is another world, a world of people who create and share: Hugging Face. It is like a great free library where thousands of people leave their tools so that anyone can use them, without programming, without installing. And you, without doing anything, can use them all.

Let us understand what Hugging Face is. It is a community on the internet where people share "models" and "Spaces". A model is the brain of an AI, the thing that has learned to do something: draw, write, speak. A Space is a ready-made tool: someone has put the model in a box with a button, and you open it and use it. It is like someone has cooked and left the dish on the table: you only serve yourself.

Why is this revolutionary for us? Because until now the tools we used were chosen by others. On Hugging Face, there are thousands, and they are free. You want a model that turns drawings into photos? There is one. A model that removes the background of a photo? There is one. A model that reads your handwriting? There is one. And they are all open, made by people who share for the love of it.

How do you use a Space? You enter huggingface.co, look for "Spaces", and choose one. Many have a small window where you put your text, your image or your sound, and a button to press. In a few seconds the result appears. There is no installation, no payment, no technical knowledge. It is the Branch in its purest form: tools that do real things, at your fingertips.

Let us try an example. There are Spaces to turn your words into images, like the ones we saw in level 8. But now they are open: someone in Brazil, someone in Japan, someone in Ghana has created a model and left it there. You use it, and if you want, you leave a thank you. It is like a neighbourhood where everyone leaves a dish for everyone else.

Another example: the audio. There are Spaces that read a text aloud, with voices that sound real. You write "Good morning, neighbours, tomorrow we have the meeting", and the Space speaks it for you. It is useful for sending a voice note to the group, or for someone who finds it hard to read. The voice of the Branch is amazing, and in the following levels we will go deeper.

And another: the images. There are Spaces that improve old photos, that colour them, that repair a torn picture. Think of your family album: a photo of your grandparents, yellowed by time. You upload it to a Space, and it comes back clear and in colour. The people who created it did not know you, but they made it for you. That is the beauty of sharing.

Now, an important lesson: not everything is perfect. As it is a community, the tools are made by many different people: some work wonderfully, others are experiments that still fail. If a Space does not work, do not worry: there are a thousand more. You learn to try. It is like a market: you try the melon, and if it is not good, you try another.

And a lesson of confidence: you do not need to understand everything. You open, try, and use. If you understand the idea of a model and a Space, you already know enough to walk through this world. The details belong to those who create them; to you belongs the use. And use is the door that opens everything.

Let us look at the ethical dimension that this world teaches us. Behind each Space there is a person who has spent time, and often shares it for free. When you use it, be grateful, and if something is wrong, tell them kindly. And remember that open does not mean that there are no rules: what is open is used with respect. The community is a garden: everyone waters it.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The voice for the announcement**: the community centre wants to hang a voice announcement at the door. You use a voice Space: you write the text and it reads it. You download the audio and put it on the mobile phone. No studio, no microphone, no embarrassment.
2. **The restored photograph**: in the album there is a photo of your mother as a child, damaged. You find a Space that repairs photos, you upload it, and in a few seconds you have it clear and in colour. You print it and give it as a gift. Magic made by people who share.
3. **The translated leaflet**: the neighbours of the association need the leaflet in three languages. You use a translation Space, or the one you already know from level 7. You copy, you paste, and you print. The tools of the community serve the community.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open huggingface.co and look at the main page. Observe: it is not like the tools we knew; it is a community.
2. Look for the "Spaces" section. Look at how many there are and who created them.
3. Choose one of the "featured" Spaces, the recommended ones. Open it.
4. Look at the window: how is it used? What does it ask for? Text, an image?
5. Try it: put something of yours, press the button, wait. Look at the result.
6. Try a second Space, of a different type: for example one of images if the first was of text.
7. If one does not work, do not give up: close it and open another. There are thousands.
8. Write in your notebook: which Space have you used and what has it done for you?
9. Think: how could this tool help someone in your life? Share it.

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is Hugging Face and what do people share there?
2. What is a Space and how do you use it?
3. What is a model, in simple words?
4. What can you do if a Space does not work?
5. What lesson about sharing does this world teach us?

**Answers:** 1. A community where people share AI tools, like a great free library. 2. A ready-made tool: you open it, use it and do not install anything. 3. The "brain" of an AI: the thing that has learned and produces the result. 4. Do not worry: close it and open another, there are thousands. 5. That behind each tool there are people who share, and they are treated with respect and gratitude.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
The world of Hugging Face teaches us the ethics of sharing. People create and give for free, and respect is the currency of that community. Do not use a tool to mock, to deceive or to harm others. Ask for permission if you use a Space with photos of people. And remember: open does not mean without rules; it means open to everyone who enters with good faith. When you use the work of others, be grateful. The garden grows when everyone waters it.
