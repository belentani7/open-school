# Module 2: Tools Without Code — Level 20
## Language: EN · Difficulty: Crown
## Estimated time: 5 hours

## 🎯 Level objective
- Understand that you can create your own assistants, without programming.
- Learn what a custom assistant is and what parts it has.
- Create a personal assistant for a task of your life.
- Teach it your way of speaking and your needs.
- Go from using other people's tools to creating your own.

## 📖 Essential vocabulary
| Term | Simple explanation |
|------|--------------------|
| Custom assistant | An AI that you have created for a task of yours, like a helper you have trained. |
| Instructions | What you tell it at the beginning so that it knows how to behave: its character. |
| Knowledge | The material you give it so that it knows your things: documents, notes, data. |
| Name | The name you give your assistant, so that it is yours. |
| Test | Try it with a real case and correct it: an assistant is made by testing. |
| Configure | Set the parts without programming: you choose, you connect, you adjust. |

## 📚 Main lesson
We have arrived at the Crown, and the Crown is the top of the tree: from here you can see everything. And the Crown has a marvellous gift: until now you have used the tools that others created. From today you create your own. Not by programming: by configuring. You create a custom assistant, you teach it your way, and it works for you. The person who used to ask, now builds.

Let us understand what a custom assistant is. You already know the chatbots: you open them and you ask. A custom assistant is the same, but made by you: with a name, a character, your knowledge and your tasks. It is like hiring a helper, but instead of giving him a contract, you give him instructions. The big companies let you do it: ChatGPT has "custom GPTs" and Gemini has "Gems".

What parts does it have? Three. The instructions: what you tell it so that it behaves, like its character: "you are an assistant for my garden, you speak simply, you remind me of the tasks". The knowledge: the material you give it, documents with your things: "here are the rules of the community". And the way: how it answers you, short or detailed, formal or familiar. Three keys, and with three keys you already create.

Let us see a real example. You are in the association of the neighbourhood and every month you have to do the same: the minutes, the reminders, the notices. You create an assistant called "The secretary". You tell it in the instructions: "You are the secretary of the association, you write clear minutes, you remind me of the monthly tasks, you speak in a simple way." You give it the rules of the centre. And from now on, you ask it and it helps you with everything. It is yours.

The secret is the instructions. A good assistant is born from a good instruction. We already know this from our levels: the good question. Now it is a good instruction: with role, context and limits. "You are a doctor's appointment helper for people over 60, you explain with patience, you do not invent treatments, you always say when to ask a professional." More precise, better assistant.

And the knowledge. You can give it documents: the rules, the recipes of your mother, the list of the grandchildren's allergies. The assistant reads them and answers based on them. It is like NotebookLM of level 12, but always at hand, your own. You feed it with your things, and it stops being generic to be yours.

Let us also talk about what an assistant is NOT. It is not a person, and it does not replace the people. It is not for the decisions, nor for the affection, nor for the responsibility. It is for the tasks: the reminders, the drafts, the summaries, the organised information. You decide, it helps. The same golden rule of all our levels, now in the Crown.

And the work of testing. An assistant is not born finished: it is tested. You ask it something real, you look at the answer, and you correct: "answer me shorter", "you have forgotten the dates", "use a calmer tone". Each correction makes it better. It is like teaching a child who learns fast: you do not get angry, you guide. Test, correct, test again.

Let us also speak of the pride. There is a moment that is worth everything: when your assistant answers you well for the first time, with your way, from your things. It is the same pride as when the tree gives its first fruit. Because it is not the fruit of a company: it is the fruit of your work. You have gone from asking to creating. And it was all without a single line of programming.

An advice before you start: choose one task. Do not make an assistant for everything: make one for something concrete, that repeats, that is yours. The garden, the association, the family medicine. A good assistant for one task is worth more than a perfect one for none. When it works, you make the second, and the third. One by one, the Crown fills with fruit.

And the last lesson: the Crown does not close the tree, it opens it. With your own assistants, you will want to connect them, to make them work together, to put them in your daily life. That is the next level. But first: create your first one, test it, and feel that it is yours. Welcome to the top of the tree.

## 💡 Practical examples
1. **The "secretary" of the association**: you create an assistant with the name and character of a secretary. You give it the centre's rules. From now on: "write me the minutes of today", "remind me of the monthly tasks". The association runs itself with your helper.
2. **The "garden"**: an assistant for your garden. You teach it the plants you have and their needs. You ask: "what do I do with the tomatoes in August?" It answers you from what you taught it. Your garden, understood by your assistant.
3. **The "family agenda"**: an assistant with the birthdays, allergies and schedules of the family. You ask: "what do we have next week?" and it reminds you. A family diary that never forgets, made by you.

## 🛠️ Guided activity
1. Open a tool that lets you create custom assistants: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) in the "custom GPT" part, or Gemini (gemini.google.com) in the "Gems" part.
2. Look at the screen: find the button to create a new assistant.
3. Give it a name: yours, short, that you like.
4. Write the instructions: who it is, what it does, how it speaks. Use the good question formula: role, context, limits.
5. Add knowledge if you can: a small document of yours, a note, a list.
6. Try it: ask it something real of your life.
7. Look at the answer: what would you change? Correct it: "shorter", "simpler", "remember the dates".
8. Test it again. Look at how it improves with each correction.
9. Write in your notebook: what task of your life deserves your first assistant?

## ✍️ Self-assessment exercises
1. What is a custom assistant and what parts does it have?
2. What is the instruction and why is it the secret?
3. What is the knowledge and what does it give the assistant?
4. What can an assistant NOT do?
5. How is an assistant improved?

**Answers:** 1. An AI created by you, with three parts: instructions, knowledge and way of answering. 2. What you tell it at the beginning so that it knows how to behave: the better the instruction, the better the assistant. 3. The material you give it, documents and notes: it turns it from generic into yours. 4. It does not decide for you, nor does it replace people: it helps, you decide. 5. By testing and correcting: each correction makes it better, like teaching.

## ⚖️ Ethical dimension
You are creating an assistant with your knowledge, and that power deserves care. Teach it well: do not give it false data, do not ask it to deceive, do not use it to impersonate a person. If you give it documents with data of others, protect them: your secretary must not leak the community's list. And remember the most important: the assistant helps, but the responsibility is yours. A good assistant does not take away decisions; it gives you time to take them better.
