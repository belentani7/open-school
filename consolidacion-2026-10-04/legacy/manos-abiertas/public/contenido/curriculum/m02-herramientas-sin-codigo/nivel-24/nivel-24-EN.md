# Módulo 2: Herramientas sin Código — Nivel 24
## Idioma: EN · Dificultad: Fruto
## Tiempo estimado: 3 horas

## 🎯 Objetivo del nivel
- Understand the difference between an assistant that responds and an agent that acts.
- Learn to create simple agents without programming.
- Teach an agent to do multi-step tasks for you.
- Use it with care for your paperwork, your work and your family.
- Go from asking for help to directing a small team of artificial intelligence.

## 📖 Vocabulario esencial
| Término | Explicación en palabras simples |
|---|---|
| Assistant | An AI that talks with you and answers questions (you saw it in level 20). |
| Agent | An AI that does not only answer: it does multi-step tasks using your tools. |
| Goal | The specific task you give the agent so that it achieves it. |
| Permission | What the agent can use: your documents, your email, your calendar. |
| Limit | What the agent must NOT do: no spending, no buying, no sharing. |
| No-code | Building the agent by choosing and connecting pieces, without programming. |

## 📚 Lección principal
In level 20 you created your first assistant: you gave it a name, instructions and knowledge. In level 23 you automated repetitive tasks with flows. Today comes one more step, perhaps the most beautiful: the agent. An assistant answers; an agent acts. And learning to create it, without writing a single line of code, is a big fruit of this module.

What is the difference? An assistant talks with you: you ask it "what papers do I need to renew my NIE?" and it answers with the list. An agent does not only answer: it does. It checks your documents folder, tells you which ones are missing, reminds you of the appointment and prepares the draft of the application. Several steps, several tools, all without you doing it by hand.

For those of us who come from other countries, this help is worth gold. The paperwork piles up: the NIE, the padrón, the health card, the SEPE appointments, the certificates. Each procedure has its dates and its requirements. A well-built agent becomes your personal paperwork secretary: it gathers, reminds and prepares, always under your watch.

What parts does an agent have? Four. The goal: the specific task, in one sentence. The tools: what it can use, your documents, your email, your calendar. The instructions: how it must do it, with your way of speaking and your rules. And the limits: what it must NOT do, not spend, not buy, not share, not decide without you. Four pieces, and without programming.

Where are they created? In the tools you already know. ChatGPT lets you create assistants (the "custom GPTs") and now also agents with tasks. n8n, the open-source connector from level 23, allows agents that act. Zapier has its own agents. And there are communities like Coze and Poe where you create bots without code. You choose, connect and test: that is creating without programming.

The most important thing is the instructions, and here you repeat the lesson of level 20: the good question. You give the agent a role, a context and some limits. "You are my paperwork helper. You know my renewal dates. You do not invent requirements: if you are not sure, you tell me to check with the right office. You never give my document number to anyone." The clearer the instruction, the better the agent.

Let us look at real examples. The "renewal secretary": an agent that knows which documents your folder has, warns you two months before your authorisation expires, prepares the list of what is missing and writes the application. The "home accountant": an agent that reads your notes and receipts and every month gives you the expenses summary, so that the family money reaches the end of the month. The "communicator": an agent that helps you write messages in Spanish for work, school or the neighbours' association, with a tone that you choose.

And the usual rule: you test it, correct it and protect it. Test the agent with a real case, check if it does what you asked, and correct it: "remind me with more time", "answer shorter", "do not save those documents". Each correction improves it, like training a new helper. And always watch: the agent works with your permissions, but the responsibility is yours.

The limits are your safety. An agent with access to your email and your documents is power: give it only what it needs, remove what is left over, and never give it data that could harm you if it leaked, such as account numbers or full identity documents. The agent is a good employee, not a god: you always decide what is important.

Think about what you have achieved. In level 20 you created an assistant that answers. Today you create agents that act: they do several steps for you, they gather, remind and prepare. Without programming, with your hands, for your life. From asking for help to directing a small team of artificial intelligence: that is the fruit of this level.

In the next level, the last of the module, we will put it all together: the complete no-code ecosystem, and the own judgement to know when to use it.

## 🛠️ Actividad guiada
Paso 1. Choose a task of your life that has several steps (paperwork, shopping, messages, appointments).
Paso 2. Write the goal in one sentence: "that it warns me and prepares the NIE renewal".
Paso 3. Divide it into steps: what it gathers, what it reminds, what it prepares.
Paso 4. Open a tool that lets you create agents: ChatGPT (GPTs), n8n or Zapier.
Paso 5. Create the agent and write the instructions: role, context and limits.
Paso 6. Give it permission only for what it needs: your folder, your email, your calendar.
Paso 7. Test it with a real case and check if it does the steps well.
Paso 8. Correct: change the instruction, limit what it uses, test again.
Paso 9. Protect the data and write in your notebook which limits you have set.

## ⚖️ Dimensión ética
- An agent acts with your permissions: give only what is needed and remove what is not.
- Never share document numbers or bank details without a clear reason.
- Supervise what the agent does: the responsibility for its actions is yours.
- Do not ask the agent to deceive, falsify or impersonate you.
- Warn and teach: what you learn to create you can also show to others.
