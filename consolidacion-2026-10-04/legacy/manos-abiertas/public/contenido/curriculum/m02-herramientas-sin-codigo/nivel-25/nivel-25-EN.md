# Módulo 2: Herramientas sin Código — Nivel 25
## Idioma: EN · Dificultad: Fruto
## Tiempo estimado: 3 horas

## 🎯 Objetivo del nivel
- See the whole module as an ecosystem: questions, images, voice, data, automations, assistants and agents.
- Learn to choose with judgement which tool to use in every moment.
- Build your own personal ecosystem for the home, work and the family.
- Know when technology helps and when paper and conversation are better.
- Close the module with confidence and share what you have learned.

## 📖 Vocabulario esencial
| Término | Explicación en palabras simples |
|---|---|
| Ecosystem | The set of your tools working together for your life. |
| Judgement | The ability to decide well what to use, how and when. |
| Simplify | Remove what is extra so that what you really use remains. |
| Maintain | Review your tools regularly so that they do not decay. |
| Choose | Decide calmly, knowing why you choose. |
| Share | Teach another person what you already know. |

## 📚 Lección principal
We have reached the last level of module 2. If the module were a tree, today we gather the harvest. In the Seed you learned to ask. In the Root you discovered tools that do things for you. In the Stem you began to create. In the Branch you valued the work of those who share. In the Crown you created your own assistants. And in the Fruit you put it all together: integration, automation and agents. That is the path. And you have walked it.

Today we talk about the ecosystem: the set of your tools working together, like a home where everything has its place. Your assistant for asking. Your voice for when you cannot read. Your database for what is ordered. Your automations for what repeats. Your agents for what has several steps. It is not a pile of apps: it is a team, and the coach is you.

Each person builds their ecosystem according to their life. For your home: the family agenda, the reminders for shopping and medicines. For your work: the shifts, the payslips and the appointments. For your paperwork: the NIE, the padrón, the SEPE, the renewals with their dates. For your faraway family: the calls, the photos and the messages. You do not need all the tools: you need yours.

And here judgement is born, the most valuable fruit of this module. Judgement is knowing how to choose: the right tool for each task, no more and no less. Judgement is knowing when no tool is needed at all. Judgement is not accumulating apps you do not use, not complicating what is already simple, and not trusting a machine for what needs a person.

Because there are also moments when technology is too much. The important papers are signed and also kept on paper: the work contract, the bank documents, the certificates. The conversations that matter are held face to face or by phone, with family and friends. Judgement tells you when the tool helps and when the human has no substitute. Technology is at your service, not the other way round.

The ecosystem also needs maintenance, like a garden needs watering. Once a month, a quiet hour: review your tools, remove the ones you do not use, check that your accounts are still connected with permission, update what changed and keep a copy of what is important. The digital papers are protected: the NIE photos, the renewal documents and the payslips are kept with care and with password.

Security is part of maintenance. Use a different password for each important service, or a password manager. Do not share your document numbers by message. Review the permissions you gave to the apps and remove the ones that are left over, especially those of people who no longer participate. A clean ecosystem is a safe ecosystem.

And the greatest fruit of the module is sharing. Now you know things that six months ago seemed impossible: asking an AI well, creating an assistant, automating a task, building an agent. There are people in your community, in your family, in your work who need exactly that. Teach a tool to someone this week: at the association, at work, at home. When you teach, what you know multiplies.

Look back for a moment. You have reached the end of the module with your own tools and with judgement. It does not depend on any particular app: if tomorrow a tool changes or disappears, you know how to choose another one, because what you carry inside is not the apps, it is the ability to learn. That cannot be lost, nor taken away.

You have walked the whole tree. Each level was a branch, and today the tree has fruits: yours. Do not lose sight of them: use them, take care of them and share them. Welcome to the Fruit. And the best: the road does not end here. In module 3, "AI Applied to Data", you will learn to understand and take care of the data that is behind everything you have created.

## 💡 Ejemplos prácticos
1. **The "home ecosystem":** family agenda, medicine reminders and shopping alerts, all linked and working on their own.
2. **The "paper folder":** the NIE and its renewals scanned, the calendar of dates and a backup copy in the cloud.
3. **The "craft of sharing":** one afternoon at the association, you teach three neighbours to create their first assistant.

## 🛠️ Actividad guiada
Paso 1. Write in your notebook the list of the tools you have learned in this module.
Paso 2. Next to each one, write the task of your life that it serves.
Paso 3. Mark the ones you really use and the ones you have without using.
Paso 4. Simplify: keep your set small. Remove what you do not use.
Paso 5. Choose your 3 or 4 essential tools: the ones that hold up your daily life.
Paso 6. Protect: use strong passwords, review the permissions and keep a copy of what is important.
Paso 7. Book in your calendar an hour a month to review your ecosystem.
Paso 8. This week, teach a tool to a person in your community or family.
Paso 9. Write in your notebook the three rules of your personal judgement.

## ✍️ Ejercicios de autoevaluación
1. What is the personal ecosystem? a) The set of your tools working together. b) A type of garden. c) An app.
2. What is judgement? a) The ability to decide well what to use, how and when. b) A SEPE document. c) A password.
3. When is paper better than technology? a) Never. b) When there are important papers and human decisions. c) Always.
4. What do we do with the tools we do not use? a) We keep piling them up. b) We simplify them or remove them. c) We pay for them.
5. What is the greatest fruit of the module? a) Sharing what you have learned. b) Having more apps. c) Needing nothing.

Respuestas: 1-a, 2-a, 3-b, 4-b, 5-a.

## ⚖️ Dimensión ética
- Sharing is the greatest fruit: teach another person what you have learned, without humiliating those who do not know.
- Respect other people's data: what you know about others is not shared without permission.
- Be honest with technology: say when it helps you and when it cannot do something.
- Take care of the access: remove the permissions of those who leave the community or the work.
- Technology serves you; you do not belong to it: rest, talk and live.
