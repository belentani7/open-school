# Project 4 — Product Design Challenge

## Context

An online learning product sees many new members create an account but few finish a first meaningful activity. Stakeholders have suggested reminders, a new onboarding carousel, recommendations and AI-generated study plans. None of these proposals is yet a validated solution.

## Challenge

Frame the activation opportunity, identify the highest-risk assumptions, and propose a responsible discovery and experiment plan. Create one targeted flow or prototype only after explaining what it will help learn.

| Milestone | Required artifact | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome | Behavioural outcome and quality guardrails | What change matters beyond feature delivery? |
| Opportunity | Evidence map and competing hypotheses | Which assumption is riskiest and why? |
| Prioritisation | Decision matrix with exclusions | What will deliberately not be built yet? |
| Experiment | Proportional test, signal and ethical checks | How will the team interpret either result? |
| Communication | Brief and 5-minute stakeholder readout | Are uncertainty and trade-offs clear? |

## Success criteria

The recommendation distinguishes output from outcome, combines qualitative and quantitative evidence appropriately, names privacy and inclusion risks for any AI use, and includes an explicit guardrail against harmful optimisation.

## Assessment

Use the **Research**, **Problem Definition**, **UX + AI**, **Advanced UX**, **Product Design**, and **Portfolio** rubrics. A high score requires a justified decision not to build at least one plausible proposal.
