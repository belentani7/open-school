# Project 1 — Pulse Mobile App

## Context

Pulse is a mobile habit-support concept for people who have irregular schedules and want to maintain a small health or learning routine. The product must reduce the effort of recording a completed action without pressuring users into streak-based behavior.

## Challenge

Design the smallest end-to-end mobile flow that lets a person select a meaningful habit, record progress, understand what changed, and recover from an accidental action or connection interruption.

| Milestone | Required artifact | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Assumptions map and research plan | What needs evidence before interface work? |
| Definition | Persona, journey, problem statement | Which moment is worth improving first? |
| Structure | Task flow and low-fidelity wireframes | Is the task possible without the happy path alone? |
| Prototype | Clickable mobile prototype | What behavior does this prototype test? |
| Evaluation | Two task observations and iteration log | What changed because of evidence? |

## Success criteria

The flow has a visible primary task, explicit feedback, accessible labels and focus, error recovery, a rationale for mobile choices, and a documented boundary: it does not claim to solve motivation or clinical wellbeing.

## Assessment

Use the **Research**, **Problem Definition**, **Wireframes**, **UI Design**, **Accessibility**, and **Usability Testing** rubrics. The student must include a one-page reflection on one assumption that remained unresolved.
