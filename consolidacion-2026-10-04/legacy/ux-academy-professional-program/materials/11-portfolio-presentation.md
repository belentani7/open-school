# Portfolio Presentation Template

## Seven-minute case presentation

| Minute | Message | Supporting evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | Context, audience, objective and your role | Brief or opportunity statement |
| 0:45–2:00 | Research and defining insight | Evidence, not decoration |
| 2:00–3:30 | Alternatives and design decision | Criteria and trade-off |
| 3:30–5:00 | Flow, interface, system and accessibility | Selected artifacts |
| 5:00–6:00 | Evaluation and iteration | Observation to change |
| 6:00–7:00 | Result, limitation and next learning step | Honest scope |

## Questions to rehearse

Explain one decision you would reverse with new evidence, one design constraint engineering introduced, one accessibility consideration that changed the work, and one assumption still open.
