# Reflection Guide

After every project milestone, respond in complete sentences to the prompts below.

1. What did I believe before this activity, and what evidence challenged or strengthened that belief?
2. What decision did I make, what alternatives did I set aside, and what criterion governed the choice?
3. Who could be excluded, harmed or burdened by this decision, and what safeguard is appropriate?
4. What is the smallest next action that would reduce the most important uncertainty?
5. What contribution was mine, what was collaborative, and how should this be represented honestly in the portfolio?
