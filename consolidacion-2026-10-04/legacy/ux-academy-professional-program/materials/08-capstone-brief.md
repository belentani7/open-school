# Capstone Open Brief

Choose one of the available low-risk sectors: learning access, municipal service reporting, community wellbeing, or sustainable local mobility. Do not invent participant data, test results, metrics, or stakeholder approvals.

Your evidence dossier must include research plan, findings, persona, problem statement, journey, information architecture, user flow, wireframes, high-fidelity design, system decisions, prototype, usability plan and findings, iteration log, case study, and portfolio presentation.

## Assessment gates

| Gate | Evidence | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Decision, outcome, users, constraints | Specific and ethical |
| Research | Plan and traceable findings | Method matches question |
| Design | Flows, screens, states, system | Accessible and testable |
| Evaluation | Tasks, observations, iterations | Decision follows evidence |
| Communication | Case and defense | Honest about role and limits |
