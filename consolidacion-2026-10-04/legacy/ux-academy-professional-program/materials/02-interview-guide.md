# Interview Guide Template

## Opening

Explain the purpose, voluntary nature, recording practice, withdrawal option, and how notes will be used. Confirm consent before continuing.

## Context questions

Ask about a specific past event. Prefer “Tell me about the last time…” over speculative questions.

## Core prompts

| Intent | Neutral prompt | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|
| Reconstruct behavior | Tell me about the last time you… | Sequence, context, tools |
| Understand decision | What happened next? | Trigger and trade-off |
| Clarify friction | What made that easy or difficult? | Observation and quote |

## Closing

Ask what was not covered, thank the participant, and repeat the data-handling commitment.
